TheMozambeamis an April Fools' variant of theMozambique Shotgunintroduced on2023's April Fools' Daythat utilizesEnergy Ammo. It fires three hitscan beams in the shape of a triangle after a short delay, consuming 3 ammo with each shot. The Mozambeam essentially acts like theHAVOC Rifle's single-fire mode, only usable with the now-removedSelectfire Receiverhop-up.
Being a shotgun, the Mozambeam affectsMad Maggie's movement speed because of herWarlord's IrePassive ability.
Mozambeam utilizes theOff the Gridskin by default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination the player is currently using for the Mozambique.
This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons inApex Legends, the other six being:April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun,April Fools' P2020,Mozamblam,Mozambrrr,Mozamburst, andOl' Nessie.
