TheNemesis Burst AR, orNemesis, is anassault rifleintroduced inSeason 16that utilizesEnergy Ammo. It fires in a 4-round burst but, unlike other burst weapons, the Nemesis keeps firing as long as the trigger is held, rather than being required to pull the trigger every time.
The Nemesis has an energy meter which fills up as the weapon is fired; the fire rate of the weapon will increase relative to the charge. Each burst fired will add 16.5% charge whether or not any targets are hit. After 5 seconds without being fired, charge decreases by 15% per second.[1]

## Lore
“ | The Nemesis was created by the weapons mod group known asThe Sisters, who previously helped Rampart learn the tricks of the trade. However, when Rampart left their group and began growing more successful than them, they burned down Rampart's shop. Rampart suspected her prototype of the Nemesis was lost in the fire. Unbeknownst to her, The Sisters had stolen it, and decided to release it as their own.
The Nemesis Burst AR was originally designed byRampartduring her time withThe Sisters, the gang led byBig Sister.[3]Its prototype was stolen during an attack on Rampart's shop, and it was later released by the Sisters as their first product as a licensed business.[4]

## Teasers
### Datamined and Leaked Data
The Nemesis got initially leaked in both theEvolutionCollection Eventtrailerand thepatch notesfor theSeptember 14, 2021 Patch, appearing in the list of purchasableArenasweapons using theR-301 Carbineicon as a placeholder[5]. It has been confirmed by a developer[6]that this leak was accidental, and that the weapon is "not releasing anytime soon".
The Nemesis Burst AR reappeared in another leak still using the R-301 as placeholder, this time without being compatible withStandard Stocks, and using theAuto fire mode, even though the latter seems to be a placeholder icon. The Nemesis also appeared with a charge bar, similar to theSentinelandRampage LMG, although its effects were unknown at the time, but it was known that it could be charged by simply firing the weapon.
On March 22, 2022, the Nemesis reappears once again, this time with a unique model and icon. Some of its stats were also shown, as well as being once again compatible withStandard Stocks.
