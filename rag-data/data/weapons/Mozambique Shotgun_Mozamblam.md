TheMozamblamis an April Fools' variant of theMozambique Shotgunintroduced on2023's April Fools' Daythat utilizesSniper Ammo. Unlike the Mozambique, it fires one round rather than three at once whenever the trigger is pulled. The Mozamblam essentially acts like theKraber .50-Cal Sniper.
Being a shotgun, the Mozamblam affectsMad Maggie's movement speed because of herWarlord's IrePassive ability.
Mozambeam utilizes theBrother of the Bladeskin by default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination the player is currently using for the Mozambique.
This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons inApex Legends, the other six being:April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun,April Fools' P2020,Mozambeam,Mozambrrr,Mozamburst, andOl' Nessie.
