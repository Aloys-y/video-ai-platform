## Lore
“ | The G7 Scout comes from good stock. The successor to theG2A4 Assault Rifleand theG2A5 Battle Rifle, the G7 features a simple design and parts that are easy to reproduce, making it a crowd favorite in the Outlands' most remote regions. The Scout adds a new feature to the family with its updated sniper stock, improving long-range accuracy while not sacrificing mid-range effectiveness.
The G2A6, later renamed to the G7 Scout, has been simplified from its design inTitanfall 2. Based on the M14/M1A rifle platform, the G7's drop in frame was replaced from a milled tactical stock to a solid composite body intended to serve the role as a Scout Sniper Rifle. This weapon uses 6.19x97mm LEC bullets that gives it quite a punch at mid range to long distance. When used as a DMR, your team can effectively advance and maneuver using your sight acquisition as a base of fire. It's overall mechanics haven't changed much throughout the years, as its reliability and brutal precision is a testament to its real-world inspiration.[1]

## Trivia
- The G7 Scout is the only weapon to have a rare reload animation, where the empty magazine may be ejected and then a new one is inserted both from the top. This particular reload is a reference to theM1 Garand, which theM14and, by extension, the G7 Scout, are based on.
- The G7 Scout is the only weapon whose classification changed twice, first from sniper rifle to assault rifle inSeason 4, and then from assault rifle to marksman weapon inSeason 9.
- It was originally designated as the G2A6, making it more in line with the naming scheme of itsFrontier Warancestors.
- The G7 Scout appearance is based on the realSpringfield Armory M1A SOCOM 16semi-automatic rifle fitted with aJ. Allen Enterprises JAE-100 chassis.
- The Crimson Firedrakeskin sports the phrase "You Owe Me Awe", a nod to the 1986 thrillerManhunter, in which the main antagonist Francis Dolarhyde says this line to his victim Freddy Lounds before murdering him.
- The G7 Scout isBangalore's favorite weapon. It is seen in a few of her banner poses, her character select animation, and her lobby animations.[4]
- TheSupersonicandHypersonicskins are weapon mods designed byRampart, indicated by the presence of a Rampart Mods stamp.
