ThePeacekeeperis a lever-action energyshotgunthat utilizesShotgun Shells. It fires 9 pellets in the shape of a shield, with one pellet in the middle and two on each outer line of the star shape. WhenChoke is active, the spread of the pellets is greatly reduced while aiming down sights.
The Peacekeeper can be enhanced withDisruptor Rounds, which increases damage dealt against shielded targets by 25%.
Being a shotgun, the Peacekeeper affectsMad Maggie's movement speed because of herWarlord's IrePassive ability.
