TheArcadia Supercarrieris a location inOlympus.
The Arcadia is a primarily indoor location, with the outdoor areas being mostly ramps to go inside the actual ship.
## Lore
The Arcadia was made byHammond Roboticsin 2733 onWorld's Edgeduring one of theApex Games' seasonal hiatuses. Primarily made to transport scientific equipment and supplies toOlympus, it had no apparent offensive capabilities.[1]
The Arcadia is now docked at Olympus, located above the planetPsamathe.
