TheMastiff Shotgun, orMastiff, is a semi-automaticshotgunthat utilizesShotgun Shells. It fires 8 pellets in the shape of a horizontal line, which decreases in width while aiming down the sights, reducing the spread and thus increasing accuracy.

## Pros and cons
Pros
- High damage per shot.
- Players without aLevel 5Evo Shieldcan be downed in two shots, making the time-to-kill incredibly low.
- 1× leg shot multiplier.
- Aiming down the sights reduces spread.
- Ability to reload bullets between shots.
Cons
- Animation between shots obscures vision.
- 1× head shot multiplier at 38+ meters.
- Hipfire has increased spread.This increased spread can however increase the likelihood of shots connecting.
- Slowest empty reload of all shotguns due to each shell being loaded individually.
## Lore
The M1901 Mastiff Combat Shotgun was developed specifically for low-gravity close-quarters engagements. The kinetic energy produced when fired in low-gravity can severely unbalance its user with massive recoil, so the engineers atLastimosa Armoryincorporated a muzzle velocity dampening system which mitigates that energy and greatly reduces recoil.
Intended for low to zero gravity ship boarding and clearing operations, the Mastiff gained notoriety throughoutThe Frontieras a reliable and devastating combat shotgun. Shells can be fed through the under-mounted magazine or with the optional drum magazine upgrade. Troops from theIMC's401st often refer to it as the "Mobile Claymore" as its high rate of fire proved to be devastating.[1]
