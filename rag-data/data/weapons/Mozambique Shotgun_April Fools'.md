TheApril Fools'variant of theMozambique Shotgunis a fully automatic triple-barrelshotgun pistolthat utilizesShotgun Shells. It shoots three pellets in the shape of a triangle.
When enabled, this version of the Mozambique replaces the standard version.
Being a shotgun, the April Fools' Mozambique affectsMad Maggie's movement speed because of herWarlord's IrePassive ability.
This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons inApex Legends, the other six being:April Fools' P2020,Mozambeam,Mozamblam,Mozambrrr,Mozamburst, andOl' Nessie.
