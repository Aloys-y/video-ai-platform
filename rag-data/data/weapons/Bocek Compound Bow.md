TheBocek Compound Bow,Bocek, orBow, is acompound bowintroduced inSeason 9that utilizesMythic Arrows.
Unlike other weapons, the Bocek fires an arrow when the Fire button (default://) is released. Holding the button for longer before firing will increase its damage, arrow speed, and accuracy, at the cost of slower fire rate. Pressing Reload (default:R//) while charging an arrow will un-nock it without firing. The Bocek also does not have a magazine but instead draws ammo directly from its internal ammo pool. Firing the Bocek at the right time (marked by a blue indicator below the Bocek's optic) also allows for successive shots to be charged faster, as long as they're also fired at the right time too. Missing the timing resets the charge speed to default.
Being aSupply Drop Weapon, it can only be found inCare Packages, is equipped with all of its highest rarityattachments, and cannot equip foreign ammo. It also comes pre-equipped with 60Ammo. Due to the pre-equippedShatter Caps, hipfiring the Bocek allows it to fire multiple arrows similarly to shotguns.

## Trivia
- The Bocek Compound Bow is named after David Bocek, one ofRespawn Entertainment's ex-game designers.
- The Bocek Compound Bow is one of two weapons in the game, the other being theC.A.R. SMG, with two slightly different inspect animations. The Bocek's depends on the player having arrows in their inventory or not.
- The arrows change color based on the active fire mode: blue for default fire mode, orange forShatter Caps.
- An early iteration of the Bocek can be seen equipped byLobain Part 5 ofThe Legacy AntigenQuest, as the comic was completed before the Bocek's design was finalized.
