# Fully Kitted Weapon

A Fully KittedLegendaryR-301 Carbine.
Fully Kitted Weaponsareweaponsequipped with all their possibleattachmentswhich cannot be removed, except for theoptic. If the weapon is dropped, the optic still stays on the weapon. There is no performance difference between a Fully Kitted Weapon and a weapon that has been manually equipped with the same attachments.
Fully Kitted Weapons are tieredCommon(★),Rare(★★),Epic(★★★), andLegendary(★★★★), with both the rarity and the number of the stars marking the level of the non-optic attachments a weapon has.LegendaryFully Kitted Weapons whose attachments lack aLegendaryversion default to havingEpicones, for example anR-99 SMGwill have aLaser Sightequipped.
## Distribution
### Battle Royale
InBattle Royale, all Fully Kitted Weapons areLegendary. Five weapons are selected eachSeasonto be Fully Kitted Weapons, and have a 80% chance to spawn in Hot Zones marked on anymap, even if only one can spawn in a given Hot Zone. They can also spawn in map-specific loot sources such as goldloot ticks(1% chance),Flyerscarrying gold death boxes (37.5% chance), goldcargo bots(1% chance) andloot MRVNsafter giving them their missing arm (1% chance). Some very specific locations on certain maps also have a high chance of spawning a Fully Kitted Weapon.

### Other modes
- InControl, all weapons areRareFully Kitted Weapons, and can be upgraded toEpicand thenLegendaryby earning enough Ratings to progress to the next Ratings Tier. Weapon ugrades can be skipped: for example, if aRareweapon is equipped andRatings Tier 4is reached, that weapon will becomeLegendary.
- InGun Run, all weapons are Fully Kitted Weapons, but their rarity varies depending on the weapon.
- InTeam Deathmatch, all weapons areRareFully Kitted Weapons. At some point during the match,Care Packagescontaining twoLegendaryFully Kitted Weapons and oneSupply Drop Weaponeach start dropping into the arena.
- InSeason 5'sHunts, specificLegendaryFully Kitted Weapons were available depending on the chapter.
- InAirdrop Escalation,Rare,Epic, andLegendaryFully Kitted Weapons may be found inside Care Packages. The color of the Care Package beam changes accordingly based on which type of Fully Kitted Weapons it contains.
- InArmed and Dangerous,LegendaryFully Kitted versions of certainsnipersandshotgunsmay spawn rarely across the map.
- InGold Rush Duos, all available weapons are SeasonalLegendaryFully Kitted Weapons andSupply Drop Weapons.
- InDeadeye, all non-Mythicweapons areLegendaryFully-Kitted Weapons.

## Trivia
- BeforeSeason 6,Supply Drop Weaponswere golden just like Fully Kitted Weapons. The term "gold weapon" could refer to either, and they both spawned inGold Rush Duos, a Limited Time Mode with only gold weapons. Respawn later upgraded the rarity of Supply Drop Weapons fromLegendarytoMythicto differentiate the two, and now "gold weapon" is only used for Fully Kitted Weapons.
