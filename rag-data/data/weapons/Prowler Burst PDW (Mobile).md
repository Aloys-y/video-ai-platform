## Trivia
- The Prowler is based on two realpersonal defense weapons: theFN P90and theMagpul PDR-C. It's also an homage to Seburo, Masamune Shirow's fictional arms manufacturer fromGhost in the ShellandAppleseed.
- The Prowler's icon lacks iron sights.
