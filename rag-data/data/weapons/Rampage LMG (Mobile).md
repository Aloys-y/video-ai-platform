TheRampage LMG, orRampage, is a fully-automaticlight machine gunintroduced inSeason 3that utilizesHeavy Rounds.
The Rampage has a uniqueRevved Up mechanic that consumes aThermite Grenade. It takes 4 seconds to consume the Thermite, increasing the fire rate of the Rampage by 30% for 90 seconds, even though each shot decreases the remaining charge time by 2 seconds. In this state, the Rampage can also break doors in two shots, or one if the door was already damaged.
