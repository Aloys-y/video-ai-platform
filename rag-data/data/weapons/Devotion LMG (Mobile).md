TheDevotion LMG, orDevotion, is a fully-automaticlight machine gunthat utilizes Energy Ammo.
As a Supply Drop Weapon, it can only be found in Care Packages, but is equipped with the highest tier of attachments available, including a Barrel Stabilizer, Energy Mag, Standard Stock, 2x HCOG "Bruiser", and Turbocharger.
It has a unique RPM acceleration mechanic: the longer the Devotion is fired continuously, the faster its fire rate will become, up to a cap. TheTurbochargerHop-Up allows the Devotion to reach its highest fire rate faster. As a Supply Drop Weapon, it can charge up even more quickly and skips some of the initial rev.
