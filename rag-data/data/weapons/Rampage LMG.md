TheRampage LMG, orRampage, is a fully-automaticlight machine gunintroduced inSeason 10that utilizesHeavy Rounds.
The Rampage can beRevved Up with aThermite Grenade, increasing the fire rate by 30% for 2 minutes and 27 seconds, with each shot shortening the charge by 3.5 seconds. Charging the Rampage takes 4 seconds. In this state, the Rampage can also break doors in two shots, or one if the door was already damaged.
Being an LMG, the Rampage is affected byRampart'sModded LoaderPassive ability.

## Lore
“ | It's like Ramya "Rampart" Parekh says: when you rise from the ashes of everything you've built, don't get mad - get heated. The Rampage LMG is the first gun she modded after a rival gang known asThe Sistersburnt down her shop. Taking theM600 Spitfireas a base, it features a unique intake that can be loaded with a thermite canister to amp its fire rate. Inserting a thermite and overloading the firing mechanisms allows the user to deliver powerful shots (a distinct advantage over the base Spitfire) while increasing rate of fire for a set period of time. You could say it brings the heat.

## Trivia
- The Rampage appeared in theSeason 8 Devstream. When the explosive holds were being displayed, the weapon can be seen on one of the weapon racks using a very early model.
- If a Rampage has been Revved up before, then a spent Thermite Grenade will be present in the weapon when inspected, and the grenade will be ejected from the weapon at the start of the next Revved Up animation. This is true even if the weapon was Revved Up by another player.[2]
- Ramparthas a unique animation for picking up the Rampage, where she loads the weapon more subtly than the other legends.
- TheCrackling Surgeskin for the Rampage was created as an homage toThe Gaming Merchant, anApex LegendsYouTuber. The blue and gold color scheme references his thumbnail design, and his signature farewell "Cheerio" is written on the side.[3]
- TheGrace of OdinandWrath of FenrirRampage skins are revved up with a specialThermite Grenadeskin. These, along with theFrontier FaithfulandOutlands ObliteratorC.A.R. skins are the only reactive weapon skins to have a special cosmetic of a gun attachment.
