# Rampart's Custom Modded Paintball Weapon

Rampart's Custom Modded Paintball Weapons,[1]also referred to asRampart Specials, are variants ofFully Kitted Weaponsequipped with theSplatter RoundsHop-Up, which increases their reload speed by 25% and applies a purely cosmetic paintball-like effect on projectiles, varying between yellow, purple, and green randomly.
Like Fully Kitted Weapons, optics can be removed and/or switched with other compatible optics, and the otherattachments' rarity match the rarity of the weapon when possible. Rampart Specials also feature the typical Fully Kitted Weapons HUD look, with a number of stars based on the rarity of the weapon, but with the addition of paint splatters. The splatters are also present when viewing a Rampart Special through the inventory.
Unlike Battle Royale's seasonal Fully Kitted Weapons, Rampart Specials are exclusive toBig Maude's Vend-It Machines located onWorld's Edge, requireMaterialsto be obtained, and come in various rarities likeArenas' Fully Kitted Weapons. Also, due to already being equipped with theSplatter RoundsHop-Up, other Hop-Ups that a weapon can possibly have are overridden. Prices for Rampart Specials are based on their rarity:
- Legendary:100
- Epic:75
- Rare:50.
Rampart Specials can be stolen byLobawhen using aBlack Market Boutique. Stealing one however triggers an alarm on Big Maude and the Black Market gets destroyed, dealing 10 damage to everyone near it.
Buying or stealing a Rampart Special will trigger an announcement in the shopping area by a voice clip recorded byRampart.
Rampart Specials are not available in the Vend-it Machines during theArmed and Dangerousgame mode.
