TheMozamburstis an April Fools' variant of theMozambique Shotgunintroduced on2023's April Fools' Daythat utilizesHeavy Rounds. It fires three rounds in the shape of a triangle, in a 3-round burst, totaling 9 rounds fired with one trigger pull. The Mozamburst essentially acts like theHemlok Burst AR.
Being a shotgun, the Mozambeam affectsMad Maggie's movement speed because of herWarlord's IrePassive ability.
Mozambeam utilizes theImpulse Fireskin by default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination the player is currently using for the Mozambique.
This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons inApex Legends, the other six being:April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun,April Fools' P2020,Mozambeam,Mozamblam,Mozambrrr, andOl' Nessie.
