## Pros and Cons
Pros
- High damage per shot for a pistol.This damage is further enhanced by the Skullpiercer Hop-Up, which allows it to kill with two shots to the head.
- Ammo efficient.
- Headshot dropoff is 254m.
- No movement speed penalty when aiming down sights.
- Good performance at all ranges.
- Shares the same unobstructive iron sights as the RE-45.
Cons
- Slow fire rate.
- Slow projectile speed.
- Low DPS.
- Small magazine capacity.
- A strange effect occurs with the recoil. Rather than returning to the original position after the initial kick, the gun will be slightly off its original position for a few seconds after the kick is reset, leading to less accuracy if spammed.
- Punishes inaccuracy.Can be difficult to use while under the effects ofStim.
