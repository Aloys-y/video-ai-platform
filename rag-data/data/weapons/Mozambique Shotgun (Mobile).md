TheMozambique Shotgun, orMozambique, is a fully automatic triple-barreledshotgun pistolthat utilizesShotgun Shells. It shoots three pellets in the shape of a triangle.
The Mozambique can be enhanced with theHammerpoint RoundsHop-Up, which increases damage dealt against unshielded targets by 35%.

## Pros and Cons
Pros
- Similar magazine capacity to Mastiff and Peacekeeper, and like the Peacekeeper, can reload all of its shells at once.
- Good damage if Hammerpoint Rounds are attached.
- Like the P2020, is free in Arenas.Shares the same upgrade costs as the P2020.
- Will fire from the hip automatically unlike other shotguns.
Cons
- Small pellet spread and only has three pellets.
- Relatively slow fire-rate without Shotgun Bolts.
- Low damage output without Hammerpoint, and can take an entire magazine to down a player.

## Trivia
- The name and triangular pellet pattern of the Mozambique Shotgun is a reference to theMozambique Drilltechnique of shooting someone twice in the chest and once in the head.
- Requesting optics for the Mozambique will result in the Legend asking for pistol optics, instead of shotgun ones, despite the weapon being classified as a shotgun.
