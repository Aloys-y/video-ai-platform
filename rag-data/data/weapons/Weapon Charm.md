# Weapon Charm

Weapon Charms, also calledWeapon Cosmetics, are small trinkets introduced inSeason 3that attach to the left side of aweapon. Each charm can only be affixed to one weapon at a time.
Weapon Charms can be crafted usingCrafting Metalsor purchased from theStorewithApex Coinswhen available. Certain Charms can also only be obtained through theBattle Pass,Eventsrewards, seasonalRankedrewards,Questrewards, or by leveling up.
