# Supply Drop Weapon

Supply Drop WeaponsareMythic-tierweaponsthat can only be found inCare Packages. These weapons come with a limited amount ofAmmo, and most of them are equipped with their highest tier ofAttachmentsthat cannot be removed from the weapon, along with removableOptics.
