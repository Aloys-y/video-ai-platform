TheHeart Stealeris a variant of theBocek Compound Bowintroduced inSeason 15that utilizesMythic Arrows, exclusive to theDate NightLimited Time Takeover. It only spawns in specific spots of the map, marked by a magenta beam, pointing at aLoot Tick. Breaking the Tick will drop a Heart Stealer. If aCare Packagewould spawn a Bocek, it spawns a Heart Stealer instead.
The Heart Stealer is functionally identical to the Bocek. However, dealing damage to enemy players will heal the player wielding it by half the damage dealt. If a player's teammate is inside the magenta circle created whenever the Heart Stealer is equipped, they also get healed.
Being aSupply Drop Weapon, it is equipped with all of its highest rarityattachments, and cannot equip foreign ammo. It also comes pre-equipped with 60Ammo. Due to the pre-equippedShatter Caps, hipfiring the Heart Stealer allows it to fire multiple arrows similarly to shotguns.
The Heart Stealer utilizes theCupid's Arrowskin and theLove StruckWeapon Charmby default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination currently in use for the Bocek.
