## Lore
The Sentinel ESR (ElectromagneticSniperRifle) is a dual-stage, electromagnetic actuated sniper rifle manufactured byParadinha Arsenal. Rounds are chambered through the rear bolt action receiver and fired with a traditional closed bolt pin. The round is then accelerated with a muzzle velocity of 5km/s through the EM Propulsion system. This delivers a projectile with highly destructive kinetic energy to defeat the immediate effects of bullet drop over distances of 2km. This gives the weapon more accessibility and a faster cycle time than theKraber .50-Cal Sniperin exchange for a little less lethality.[1]
