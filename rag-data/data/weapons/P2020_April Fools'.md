TheApril Fools'variant of theP2020is a semi-automaticpistolthat utilizesLight Rounds.
When enabled, this version of the P2020 replaces the standard version.
This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons inApex Legends, the other six being:April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun,Mozambeam,Mozamblam,Mozambrrr,Mozamburst, andOl' Nessie.
