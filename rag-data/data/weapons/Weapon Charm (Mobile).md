# Weapon Charm (Mobile)

Weapon Charmsare small trinkets that attach to the left side of aweapon. Each charm can only be affixed to one weapon at a time.
Weapon Charms can be purchased from the store usingSyndicate Gold, or obtained through theBattle Pass,Eventrewards and challenges, andPremium Packs.
