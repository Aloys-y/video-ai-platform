# Signature Weapons

Signature Weaponsare cosmetic items that change the skin of your melee weapon inApex Legends Mobile. Signature Weapons typically come in two versions, and can be customized with Textures and Effects that are unlocked as you progress with the weapon.
A Signature Weapon can be unlocked with 400 Signature Merits or 40 Premium Signature Merits. Further stages can be unlocked with Signature Merits, containing newEmoji,Holosprays, Textures, and Effects.
