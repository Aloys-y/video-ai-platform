TheL-STAR EMG, orL-STAR, is a fully-automaticenergy machine gunthat utilizesMythic Energy Ammo. It fires red plasma orb-like projectiles.
The L-STAR does not have a magazine, but instead draws ammo directly from the inventory. The L-STAR has a unique overheating mechanic, which triggers after 22 /24/26/30continuous shots; it only needs to reload if it overheats.
