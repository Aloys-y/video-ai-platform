The30-30is alever action repeating rifleintroduced inSeason 1that utilizesHeavy Rounds. It has a unique charge mechanic allowing the weapon to do more damage (up to 36% more) by aiming down sights for longer without firing. It can also be simply fired without charging at a faster fire rate.

## Trivia
- The name is a reference to the.30-30 Winchestercartridge used by theWinchester Model 1894rifle, on which the weapon's design is based.
- The 30-30 is one of the only two weapons in the game to reload one bullet at a time, the other one being theMastiff Shotgun.
