ThePeacekeeperis a lever-action energyshotgunthat utilizesShotgun Shells. It fires 11 pellets in the shape of a star, with one pellet in the middle and two on each outer line of the star shape. WhenChoke is active, the spread of the pellets is greatly reduced while aiming down sights.

## Pros and Cons
Pros
- Excellent damage if all pellets hit, enough to break Level 3 Evo Shields.
- When the choke is fully charged, very accurate and can hit enemies from similar ranges to Assault Rifles.
- Traditional pellet shape allows for more conventional shotgun tactics.
- Same magazine capacity as the Mastiff, but can reload much faster.
- Unlike the Triple Take, the choke will remain active for a period of time after lowering your weapon.
Cons
- Slow fire-rate due to lever-action
- Default iron sights are small.The choke reticle's size also depends on the sight equipped. With no sight, it is extremely small and hard to aim with.
- Punishes inaccuracy if choke is activated.
