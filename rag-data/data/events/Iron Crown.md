Iron Crownwas aCollection EventduringSeason 2ofApex Legendsthat began on August 13th, 2019 and concluded on August 27th, 2019.
The event featured the limited timeSolos Mode. It also included theIron Crown CollectionandIron Crown Event Shopthat sold event-exclusive cosmetics.

The event introduced the first Town Takeover in the form ofOctane's Gauntlet. The new area replaced West Settlements and is themed around the legendOctane.
The area consists of a gauntlet with ramps containing Jump Pads placed in the track. A giant ring of fire is located in the middle of the area which contains high-tier loot.

## Iron Crown Collection
TheIron Crown Collectionwas a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. During the start of the event until 20 August 2019, the only way to obtain these cosmetics were to open the event-exclusiveApex PackscalledIron Crown Packs(which cost700) but due to significant backlash, was also made available on theStoreon a rotating basis. Iron Crown Packs only gave out Iron Crown Collection Cosmetics andCrowns, which were the event currency.
Completion of the Iron Crown Collection allowed the player to directly purchaseBloodhound's Heirloom Setfor3,500 before it was added to the normal Apex Packs loot table, and later theMythic Store.
