Gaiden(外伝) was aThemed EventduringSeason 13ofApex Legendsthat began on July 19th, 2022 and concluded on August 2nd, 2022.
## Challenges
The event featured a set of challenges that unlocked exclusive badges. The badge and challenge available changed after each week.
Week | Challenge | Reward
Week 1 | Deal 3000 Damage with Shotguns in Any Mode during Week 1 of theGaidenEvent | Badge:Gaiden Scattershot
Week 2 | Survive 50 Ring Closings in Any Mode during Week 2 of theGaidenEvent | Badge:Gaiden Survivor

## Trivia
- The skins for the Gaiden event are based on characters from popular anime franchises:Wattson'sLightning Spiritskin is based onNaruto Uzumakifrom theNarutoseries.Mirage'sMarked Manskin is based onIzuku Midoriya / Dekufrom theMy Hero Academiaseries.Revenant'sRevelationsskin is based onEvangelion Unit-00andEvangelion Mark.09from theNeon Genesis Evangelionfranchise. It specifically references the yellow coloration of its original appearance in the early episodes ofNeon Genesis Evangelion, as well as Mark.09's appearance in theRebuild of Evangelionfilms with the scythe.TheVK-47 Flatline'sRevelationsskin is marked as "Test Type," which is the designation given toEvangelion Unit-01.Octane'sSea Legsskin is based onMonkey D. Luffyfrom theOne Pieceseries. Though originally given a straw coloration, it was edited to have a blue rim on release, likely to avoid copyright infringement.Seer'sHeart Stopperskin is based onMamoru Chiba / Tuxedo Maskfrom theSailor Moonseries.Bloodhound'sLifebloodskin is based onKen Kanekifrom theTokyo Ghoulseries.Bangalore'sSoldier of Justiceskin is based onUsagi Tsukino / Sailor Moonfrom theSailor Moonseries.Crypto'sStrength and Powerskin is based onSon Gokufrom theDragon Ballfranchise.Fuse'sDown Thunderskin is based onEdward Elricfrom theFullmetal Alchemistseries.
