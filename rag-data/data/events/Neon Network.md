Neon Networkwas aCollection EventduringSeason 17that began on July 25, 2023 and ended on August 8, 2023.
## Node Tracker Takeover
For the duration of the event, the Node Tracker Takeover replaces normal Trios and Duos.
During the Takeover, all players spawn with aNode Trackerin their Survival Item slot. Equipping the Node Tracker will direct the player towards a nearby Compute Node zone. Entering the zone while holding the Node Tracker will play a brief upload animation, after which aLoot Tickwill appear and the player is granted 875-900Nodes. This can only be done once per game. Nodes can be used to purchase items in theReward Shop, and the cumulative amount of Nodes collected unlocks scenes fromKill Code: Interludein theStory Progression.
Players can only receive Nodes up to five times per day, though they can still use the Node Tracker and receiveLoot Ticksas often as desired.

## Neon Network Collection
TheNeon Network Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics could be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveNeon Network collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Neon Network Collection allowed the player to unlockValkyrie's Prestige Skin Set, before it was added to theMythic Store.
