Voidwalkerwas aThemed EventduringSeason 2ofApex Legendsthat began on September 3rd, 2019 and concluded on September 17th, 2019.
The event featured certain challenges, a town takeover, new skins, and a limited timegame mode,Armed and Dangerous.
