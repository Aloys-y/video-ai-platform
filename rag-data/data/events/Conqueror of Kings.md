Conqueror of Kingsis an event forSeason 2ofApex Legends Mobile.
## Event Details
Players will need to complete challenges at specific locations onKings Canyon. Each location has three challenges, with a new location being unlocked daily. After completing all three challenges, the player will earn 10Conqueror Points, which will allow them to earn rewards.
## Challenges
- Encore Galore- Artillery- Slum Lakes- Deal 1000 damage at Encore Galore.- Kill 5 enemies at Encore Galore.- Deal 500 damage in a single match at Encore Galore.- Deal 1000 damage at Artillery.- Kill 5 enemies at Artillery.- Open 5 Supply Bins at Artillery.- Deal 1000 damage at Slum Lakes.- Kill 5 enemies at Slum Lakes.- Land at Slum Lakes at the start of the match.
