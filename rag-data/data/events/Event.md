# Event

There are three main types ofeventsinApex Legends:Seasonlaunches, Themed Events, and Collection Events.[1]Minor events also exist.
## Season Launches
Main article:Season
These are to coincide with the launch of a newSeasonand will include a newLegend, a majormapupdate, new loot items (weapons, ordnances, etc.), and potentially new features. These events also include significant balance changes.

## Apex Mobile Events
Cosmetic Events inApex Legends Mobiletend to fall into three categories:
### Store Vault
During a Store Vault event, players can spend140 to attempt to obtain an event item from the loot pool. Duplicate items are converted intoStore Vault Tokens, which can be redeemed for event cosmetics. After opening the box a certain amount of times, the player can unlock milestone rewards
#### Store Vault Events
- Extreme Speed
- Winter Warfare
- Mecha Strike Go!
- Rhapsody's Underground
- Future Wave
- Awoken Nightmares
- Royal Regiment
### Arsenal Drop
During an Arsenal Drop, also known as a Versus Drop, a player can spend45 to obtain an item at random. A row will be selected, with each row having a different probability. Duplicate items cannot be obtained.
#### Arsenal Drop Events
- Heat Wave
- Glacial Games
- Demolition Crew
- Neon Rebellion
- Infernal Rager
- Nightclub Icons
- Digital Bliss Fest
- Shipwrecked
- Secrets of the Tomb
- Barbarian Invasion
### Phase Heist
During a Phase Heist, players can spend30 to obtain a random reward from the loot pool. If aCoordinates Chipis obtained, the player will be rewarded a cosmetic from the Phase Vault loot pool.
#### Phase Heist Events
- Golden Geometry
- Frontline Battalion
- Pythas Error Control
- Party Animal
- Divinity's Blessing

- Fight or FrightandHolo-Day Bashare the only major events that return. They both were initially released as Collection Events and came back as Themed Events.
- The song used in theWar Gamestrailer is sang byFuse's voice actor, Ben Prendergast.
- According to the officialevent structure overviewreleased back inSeason 2, possible extensions toApex Legends'lore is a feature of Themed Events. However, as ofSeason 7, this appears to be no longer the case since theFight Nightevent, a Collection Event, came with aStory from the Outlands.
- Events are sometimes further categorized beyond Themed and Collection:Grand Soiréeis also called anArcade Event;The Old Waysis also called aLore Event. (By this logic,VoidwalkerandFight Nightshould also be Lore Events.)
- The Battle Armor event was not a planned event. It was a last-minute addition after the launch ofSeason 5got moved back one week.
- Some events come with a special event-themedMusic Pack. There are some exceptions.Grand Soirée,Fight Night,Anniversary, andWar Gamesnever got their own music packs.The returning EventHolo-Day Bash (2020)had no "new" music pack, but had the same as the originalHolo-Day Bash (2019)Christmas-themed music pack.However, the other returning EventFight or Fright (2020)had an own music pack and did not share the same withFight or Fright (2019).Voidwalkerhad no Music Pack of its own, but "shared" and released the music pack ofWraith.Legendary Hunthad its own themed music, but it was not rewarded as a selectable music pack because Music Packs didn't exist inSeason 1.
