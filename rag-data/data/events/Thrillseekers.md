# Thrillseekers

ThrillseekersStart date | Jul 13 2021
End date | Aug 03 2021
Duration | 21 days
During | File:Season.svgSeason
Thrillseekerswas aThemed EventduringSeason 9ofApex Legendsthat began on July 13th, 2021 and concluded on August 3rd, 2021. It also featured theSummer Splash Salethat ran from July 13 to July 27.
## Overflow
With the event came a newArenasmap,Overflow.
## Challenges
The event featured a set ofArenas-specific challenges that would unlock exclusive badges. The badge and challenge available changed after each week.
Week | Challenge | Reward
Week 01 | Play 20 Arena Games Total. | Badge:Thrillseekers Athlete
Week 02 | Get 100 Knockdowns Total in Arenas. | Badge:Thrillseekers Pugilist
Week 03 | Deal 12,000 Damage Total in Arenas. | Badge:Thrillseekers Damage Dealer
### Rewards
