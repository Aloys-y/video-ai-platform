# Unshackled

UnshackledStart date | April 19 2022

During | File:Season.svgSeason
Gamemode | Flashpoint
Unshackledwas aThemed EventduringSeason 12ofApex Legendsthat began on April 19th, 2022 and concluded on May 3rd, 2022.
## Challenges
The event featured a set ofBattle Royale-specific challenges that unlocked exclusive badges. The badge and challenge available changed after each week.
Week | Challenge | Reward
Week 1 | Get 50 Knockdowns in BR during Week 1 of the Unshackled Event | Event Badge:Unshackled Rage
Week 2 | Deal 5,000 Damage in BR during Week 2 of the Unshackled Event | Event Badge:Unshackled Assault
