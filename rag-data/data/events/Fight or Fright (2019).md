Fight or Frightwas aCollection EventduringSeason 3ofApex Legendsthat began on October 15th, 2019 and concluded on November 5th, 2019.
## Shadowfall
Main article:Shadowfall
Shadowfall is a limited-timegame modeset in the night-time of an abandonedKings Canyon. In this mode, 35 solo players drop into the map, where ashadowy figurehas hosted a twisted version of theApex Games. Killed players in this mode becomeShadows, who then gain increased speed, have more powerful melee attacks and are instantly respawned upon death. When only ten players are remaining, they must escape Kings Canyon in order to win the match, otherwise the Shadows will win.

## Fight or Fright Collection
TheFight or Fright Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveFight or Fright Packsfor700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Following the controversy surrounding theIron Crownevent, the completion of the Fight or Fright Collection now allowed the player to unlockLifeline's Heirloom Setfor free before it was added to the normal Apex Packs loot table, and later theMythic Store.
