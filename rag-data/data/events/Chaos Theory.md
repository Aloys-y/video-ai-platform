Chaos Theorywas aCollection EventduringSeason 8ofApex Legendsthat began on March 9th, 2021 and concluded on March 23rd, 2021.
## Nintendo Switch
With the event came the release ofApex LegendsonNintendo Switch. From March 9th to May 4th, 2021, players who play on Switch were able to get the newP.A.T.H.Pathfinderskin as well as 30 freeSeason 8Battle Pass Levels. Additionally, Switch players were able to earn Double XP for the first two weeks after launch on March 9th (the event’s duration).
## No-Fill Matchmaking
”Fill Teammates” option.
With the event came the release of a new no-fill matchmaking feature. A checkbox saying "Fill Teammates" will appear above the game mode selection screen if you're playing Duos or Trios. Unchecking this box and clicking Play will send your party into your selected playlist without filling your team with other players. After every match, it will automatically be checked. Also, the server only allows 6 no-fill players in a single match and the featured isn't available inRanked Leagues.

## Ring Fury
Ring Fury Icon
Ring Furyis a takeover of the normal Play Apex game mode, where an increasing amount of Ring Flares (smaller rings) spawn around the active playspace in addition to the primary ring which behaves as usual. Those who get caught in a Ring Flare take damage equal to the damage dealt by the current round’s Ring. You’ll be given a short warning on the map and minimap before the Ring Flare appears. Legends will call out if they are in direct danger or if there’s one opening up nearby. It's an escalation takeover, meaning that the number of Ring Flares will increase as the event goes on. A new item, theHeat Shield, protects legends from that Ring damage for a period of time before disappearing. Players also get 25% faster revives while in the shield, as long as the Ring is above it.

## Chaos Theory Collection
TheChaos Theory Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveChaos Theorypacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Chaos Theory Collection allowed the player to unlockBangalore's Heirloom Setbefore it was added to theMythic Store.
