Apex Legends X Post Malonewas aThemed EventduringSeason 19ofApex Legendsthat began on November 7, 2023 and ended on November 21, 2023. This event marked the first appearance ofIconicSkins.
## Reward Shop
During the event, players could earnCamo Creditsby completing daily challenges. These could be exchanged for rewards in the Reward Shop, and the cumulative amount of Camo Credits earned unlocked a series of badges.
