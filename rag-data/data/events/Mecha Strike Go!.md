# Mecha Strike Go!

Mecha Strike Go!Start date | July 12, 2022
End date | August 24, 2022
Duration | 42 days
During | File:Season.svgSeason
Mecha Strike Go!is a Store VaultEventforSeason 2ofApex Legends Mobile.
## Store Vault
During the Store Vault event, players can spend140 to attempt to obtain an event item from the loot pool. Duplicate items are converted intoStore Vault Tokens, which can be redeemed for event cosmetics.
After opening the box a certain amount of times, the player can unlock milestone rewards:
Openings | Reward
5 | 20050Store Vault Tokens
15 | 250200Store Vault Tokens
35 | 300500Store Vault Tokens
65 | 5001000Store Vault Tokens
125 | Avatar frame1500Store Vault Tokens
