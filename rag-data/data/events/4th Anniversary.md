4th Anniversaryis aCollection EventduringSeason 16ofApex Legendsthat began on February 14th, 2023 and ended on February 28th, 2023.
## Log-In Rewards
In celebration of the fourth birthday ofApex Legends, all players received rewards upon logging in.
- Week 1 (February 14 - February 21): UnlockCryptoand aThematic Pack.
- Week 2 (February 22 - February 28): UnlockAshand aThematic Pack.
Logging in at any point during the event rewarded players with theAnniversaryMusic Pack.

## 4th Anniversary Collection
The4th Anniversary Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusive4th Anniversary collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the 4th Anniversary Collection rewards the player with [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150.
