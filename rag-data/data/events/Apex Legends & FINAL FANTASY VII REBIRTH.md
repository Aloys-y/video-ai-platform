Apex Legends & FINAL FANTASY™ VII REBIRTHis aThemed EventduringSeason 19ofApex Legendsthat began on January 9, 2024 and will end on January 30, 2024.
## Cosmetics
The primary cosmetic set features 36 cosmetics. All can be obtained by openingFFVII Rebirth Event Packs, whileIconicskins are also purchasable for2,150 each.
TheBuster Sword R5Melee Weapon skin can additionally be obtained from event packs, or as a guaranteed reward after collecting all 36 cosmetics.
