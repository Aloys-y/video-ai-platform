Evolutionwas aCollection EventduringSeason 10ofApex Legendsthat began on September 14th, 2021 and concluded on September 28th, 2021.
## Town Takeover
Located near Lava City onWorld's Edge,Rampart's Big Maudeis aRampart-themedtown takeover. It features a new shop that Rampart built along with a paintball arena on the outside. Inside the shop players can find vent-it machines that allow them to buy modded weapons with theSplatter Roundshop-up. These weapons have larger magazines and reload faster. Additionally, inArenas, there are special variants of weapons that can be purchased with a discountedMaterialsprice.

## Evolution Collection
TheEvolution Collectionwere a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics  were obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveEvolutionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Evolution Collection allowed the player to unlockRampart's Heirloom Setbefore it was added to theMythic Store.
