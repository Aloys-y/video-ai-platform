## 5th Anniversary Collection
The5th Anniversary Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusive5th Anniversary collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the 5th Anniversary Collection rewards the player with [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150.
