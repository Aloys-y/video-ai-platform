Anniversarywas aCollection EventduringSeason 8ofApex Legendsthat began on February 9th, 2021 and concluded on March 2nd, 2021.
## Locked and Loaded Takeover
Locked and Loaded is a takeover of the normal Play Apex game mode. All players start the game with aMozambique Shotgunwith aShotgun Boltand a1x HCOG "Classic". Players will also spawn with 2Shield Cells, 2Syringes, anEvo Shield,Helmet,Knockdown Shield, andBackpack. All other floor loot is onlyRaretier and above. NoCommonitems, except for 1x optics. The spawn rates ofCare Package Weaponsis higher in this game mode, andSeason 8Fully Kitted Weaponsalso spawn in Care Packages in this mode.

## Anniversary Collection
TheAnniversary Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveAnniversarypacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using1,200 forLegendaryitems and400 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Anniversary Collection gives players [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150 to choose an existingMythicset of their choice, even if the player has all the heirloom sets unlocked.

## Trivia
- This event marked the second anniversary ofApex Legendsdespite not being released on the actual anniversary of February 4th.
- This event was accidentally released early, on February 2nd, but was patched out minutes later.
- This event was the first event to get extended, as it got extended by a week, from February 23rd to March 2nd, 2021.
