# Wintertide

Wintertideis aCollection EventduringSeason 15ofApex Legendsthat began on December 6, 2022 and ended on December 27, 2022.
## Challenges
The event featured a set of specific challenges that unlocked exclusive badges. The badge and challenge available changed after each week.
Week | Challenge | Reward
Week 1 | Deal 15,000 damage to enemy players in Any Mode during the Wintertide Event | Badge:Wintertide Avalanche
Week 2 | Get 50 Kills or Assists in Any Mode during the Wintertide Event | Badge:Wintertide Wipe Out
Week 3 | Restore 2,500 Health in Any Mode during the Wintertide Event | Badge:Wintertide Restoration

## Wintertide Collection
TheWintertide Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics could be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveWintertide collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Wintertide Collection allowed the player to unlockWraith's Prestige Skinprior to being added to theMythic Store.
