# September Soirée

TheSeptember Soiréewas aminor eventforSeason 6ofApex Legendsthat ran from September 8th, 2020 to October 6th, 2020. It consisted of a rotating set of limited-timegame modesfrom September 8th to October 6th, and a Sale that took place from September 15th to September 22nd. TheSeptember Soiree Salefeatured 3 bundles containing skins from theGrand Soiréeevent and 4 Apex Packs bundles.
