## Beast of Prey Collection
TheBeast of Prey Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics will be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveBeast of Preypacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Beast of Prey Collection allowed the player to unlockLoba's Heirloom Setprior to being added to theMythic Store.
