## Spellbound Collection
TheSpellbound Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics could be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveSpellbound collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Spellbound Collection allowed the player to unlockSeer's Heirloom, theShowstoppers, before it was added to theMythic Store.
