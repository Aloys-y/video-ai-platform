## Warriors Collection
TheWarriors Collectionwas a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics were obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveWarriorspacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Warriors Collection allowed the player to unlockCrypto's Heirloom Setprior to being added to theMythic Store.
