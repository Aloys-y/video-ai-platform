# Valentine's Day Rendezvous

| This article is about the 2020 event. For the 2019 event, seeValentine's Day.
Valentine's Day Rendezvouswas aminor eventforSeason 4ofApex Legendsthat ran from February 12th, 2020 to February 19th, 2020. It released two new items exclusive to the event's time period that follow the theme of Valentine's Day, as well as two returning items.
This Valentine's event also featured the return ofDuosgame mode. DoubleXPwas available to those who queued up with a friend (up to 20k per day) during the time of the event.
## Challenges
- Valentines 2020BadgeRequirement: Log-in between February 12 - February 19 2020.
