Fight or Frightwas aThemed EventduringSeason 14ofApex Legendsthat began on October 4th, 2022 and ended on November 1st, 2022.
## Game Modes
Over the course of the event, various rotating game modes were featured:
- October 4 - October 11:Shadow RoyaleonOlympus After Dark
- October 11 - October 18:Gun RunonEstates After Dark,Skull Town, andFragment East
- October 18 - October 25:ControlonLava Siphon,Barometer, andHammond Labs After Dark
- October 25 to November 1:Shadow RoyaleonOlympus After Dark
