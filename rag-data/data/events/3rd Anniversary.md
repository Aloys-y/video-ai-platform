3rd Anniversarywas aCollection EventduringSeason 12ofApex Legendsthat began on February 15th, 2022 and concluded on March 1st, 2022.
## Log-In Rewards
In celebration of the third birthday ofApex Legends, all players received rewards for three weeks. For new players, each week allowed them to obtain aLegend, and for players that already had them, the rewards also included a total of 10Apex Packs.
- Week 1 (February 8 - February 15): UnlockOctaneand 3Thematic Packs.
- Week 2 (February 15 - February 22): UnlockWattsonand 3Thematic Packs.
- Week 3 (February 22 - March 1): UnlockValkyrie, 3Thematic Packsand 1Legendary Pack.

## 3rd Anniversary Collection
The3rd Anniversary Collectionwas a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics were obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveAnniversarypacks for700 which would give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpicitems.
Completion of the Anniversary Collection allowed the player to unlockBloodhound's Prestige Skin Setbefore it was made available for purchase in theMythic Store.
