# Neon Rebellion

Neon RebellionStart date | July 22 2022
End date | August 9 2022
Duration | 18 days
During | File:Season.svgSeason
Neon Rebellionis a Versus DropEventforSeason 2ofApex Legends Mobile, focusing onCausticandRhapsody.
## Versus Drop
During a Versus Drop, a player can spend45 to obtain an item at random. A row will be selected, with each row having a different probability. Duplicate items cannot be obtained.
