# Rampart Arenas Takeover

Rampart Arenas Takeover(also calledRampart's Arenas Extravaganza) is aLimited Time TakeoverforArenasintroduced inSeason 10'sEvolutionevent.
## Details
- Basic gameplay is the same asArenas.
- At the beginning of every match, a selection ofweaponsare chosen to be Rampart's Special Deals. These weapons can only be purchased inLevel 4,fully kittedform, with theSplatter Roundshop-upattached if applicable.Lower-tier versions of these weapons cannot be purchased, but the cost of a Special Deal weapon is 100-200creditsfewer than the cumulative cost of theLevel 3variant.SometimesSupply Drop Weaponswill be selected, in which case they will become purchasable as-is in the buy menu.Some matches will have smaller selections of weapons, but rotate selections every three rounds.
- Maps available arePhase Runner,Party Crasher,Overflow, andOlympus-based mapsGolden Gardens,Velvet Oasis, andAutumn Estates.
## Availability
- September 14th, 2021 - September 28th, 2021
