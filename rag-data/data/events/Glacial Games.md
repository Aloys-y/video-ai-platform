# Glacial Games

Glacial GamesStart date | June 18 2022
End date | July 2 2022
Duration | 26 days
During | File:Season.svgSeason
Glacial Gamesis an Arsenal DropEventforSeason 1.5ofApex Legends Mobile, focusing onLobaandPathfinder.
## Arsenal Drop
During an Arsenal Drop, a player can spend45 to obtain an item at random. A row will be selected, with each row having a different probability. Duplicate items cannot be obtained.
