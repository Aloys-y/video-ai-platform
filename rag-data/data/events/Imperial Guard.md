## Imperial Guard Collection
TheImperial Guard Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics could be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveImperial Guard collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using1,200 forLegendaryitems and400 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Imperial Guard Collection allowed the player to unlockWraith's second Heirloom Set, includingHope's Dawn, before it is added to theMythic Store.
