Genesiswas aCollection EventduringSeason 9ofApex Legendsthat began on June 29th, 2021 and concluded on July 13th, 2021.
## Legacy Maps
Legacy Mapsis a takeover of the normal Play Apex game mode, where the normal Season 9mapsin rotation (OlympusMU1 andWorld's EdgeMU2) are replaced with the original incarnations ofKings Canyonand World's Edge.
Additionally, Skull Town is anArenasmap for the duration of the event, and unlike the other maps, it stayed for 60 minutes at a time.

## Genesis Collection
TheGenesis Collectionwas a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics were obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveGenesispacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Genesis Collection allowed the player to unlockRevenant's Heirloom Setbefore it was added to theMythic Store.
