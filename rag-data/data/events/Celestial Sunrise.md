## Celestial Sunrise Collection
TheCelestial Sunrise Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics could be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveCelestial Sunrise collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Celestial Sunrise Collection allowed the player to unlock the reactiveJadeite RetributionPeacekeeperskin,Happy New YearHolospray, andCrystal SigilCharm.
