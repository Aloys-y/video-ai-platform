## Sun Squad Collection
TheSun Squad Collectionis a set of 24 cosmetics exclusive to the event. The collection cosmetics can be obtained by:
- Purchasing event-exclusiveSun Squad collectionpacks for700 which will give out one collection cosmetic and two cosmetics from the normal loot table.
- Unlocking using2,400 forLegendaryitems and800 forEpicitems.
- By direct purchase from theStore:1,800 forLegendaryitems and1,000 forEpics.
Completion of the Sun Squad Collection allowed the player to unlockAsh's Heirloom, theStrongest Link, before it was added to theMythic Store.
