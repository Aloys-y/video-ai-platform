# Perk

Perksare additional attributes provided toLegendsandLevel 4loot.
## Legend Perks
### Executioner
Finishersfully regenerateshields.Applies to allLegends.
### Fortified
Reduces damage taken on the body by 15% and provides immunity to slowness from incoming fire.Applies toFortifiedLegends:Caustic,Gibraltar,Newcastle.
## Class Perks
### Assault
Increasesammostacks in inventory; can get extra loot fromWeapon Supply Bins.Applies toAssaultLegends:Bangalore,Fuse,Ash,Mad Maggie,Ballistic.
### Skirmisher
Can reveal the most valuable item from a neutralCare Package, even if it was taken by another team.Applies toSkirmisherLegends:Pathfinder,Wraith,Octane,Horizon,Valkyrie,Revenant.
### Recon
ScanningSurvey Beaconsreveals the current position of all enemies on the map.Applies toReconLegends:Bloodhound,Crypto,Seer,Vantage.
### Support
Expired Banner Cards can be crafted atReplicators; can get extra loot fromExtended Supply Bins.Applies toSupportLegends:Conduit,Gibraltar,Lifeline,Loba,Newcastle,Mirage.
### Controller

## Loot Perks
### Autoloader
Automatically reloads holstered weapons after 3 seconds, 4 seconds if the mag is less than 50%.L-STAR EMGcan fire 6 more bullets (102 damage) before overheating.Applies to theExtended Mags.
### Deep Pockets
Increases largeRegenitem stack size by 1.Applies to theBackpack.
### Fast Charge
ReducesTacticalandUltimatecooldown time by 20%.Applies to theHelmet.
### Guardian Angel
Reviving a Bleeding Out teammate will give them 50shieldsand 75healthinstead of 0shieldsand 25health.Applies to theKnockdown Shield.
### Improved Minor Heal
Shield CellandSyringesheal 50 instead of 25.Applies to theBody Shield.
### Kinetic Feeder
Passively adds rounds one by one to the weapon every 0,5 seconds while sliding, regardless if equipped or holstered.Applies to theShotgun Bolt.

## Removed Perks
### Fast Use
Regenitems take half as long to use.Was applied to theBody Shield.
### Flash Hider
Minimizes muzzle flash.Was applied to theBarrel Stabilizer.
### Low Profile
Increases damage taken by 5% and damage taken to the legs is not reduced.Was applied toLow ProfileLegends:Lifeline,Wattson,Wraith.
### Resurrection
Allows a Bleeding Out Legend to revive themself. An item with this Perk turns into aLevel 3item once the self-revive is completed.Was applied to theKnockdown Shield.
