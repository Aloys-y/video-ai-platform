Legacy of a Thiefis aStories from the Outlandsanimated short released on April 30, 2020, to promoteSeason 5.
## Synopsis
After the death ofherparents,Loba Andradeis adopted by a new family. True to her lineage, Loba maintains an interest in theft, and hones her craft throughout the years until becoming one of the best thieves inthe Outlands. Along the way, she becomes skilled at combat with a bo staff, acquires a rare jump drive bracelet, and makes powerful allies such asJaime, who becomes her go-to informant. One day, Jaime drops the news that he's found the simulacrum who killed her parents:Revenant. Seeing his face again after so many years, Loba immediately sets her sights on revenge.
