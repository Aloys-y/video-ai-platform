The First Shipwas aquestduringSeason 6.
## Treasure Pack Rewards
PlayingDuos,Trios, orRanked Leagues, players could findTreasure Packsin-game, once per day (players could also have purchased all the available Treasure Packs for25).
Every seven Treasure Packs collected unlocked5000,15, anApex Pack, anEpicCharm, and a newStory Pagealong with anotherEpicCharm, respectively.
There were seven Story Pages during the quest, one released per week, needing a total of 43Treasure Packsto unlock - Page 1 was unlocked after the firstTreasure Packswas collected.
After allStory Pageswere collected and read, the player would receive two rewards: theTeam DynamicsM600 SpitfireSkin and theRide ShareCharm.
