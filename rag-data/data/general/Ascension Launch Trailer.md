OnWorld's Edge,Miragetries to film himself while the Arcadia is launching.Rampartreprimands him for putting her stuff on that ship[2], and the duo are forced to follow the Arcadia withPathfinderpiloting agoblindropship to warp intoPsamathe, where they stumble on the floating city ofOlympus. However, the automated defense systems opens fire on them; the Goblin's hull is torn apart, and Mirage and Rampart are flung out of it while the dropship hurtles towards the Rift.
As Mirage lands in Olympus' Golden Gardens, he stumbles to aTridentwhere he seesHorizonas the driver and Pathfinder as a passenger. As the dropship arrives and the match starts, Horizon tosses Mirage aFlatline, and the trio fight together against many squads, including a squad made of Crypto, Bangalore, and Bloodhound whom they eliminate using Horizon's gravity lift.
Still traveling on their trident, Mirage sees Rampart charging aSentinel, evidently assigned to another squad. The shot she fires damages the Trident, so the trio are forced to get off to take on a squad composed of Octane, Wraith, and Wattson by tackling them off their trident and using Horizon's Black Hole ability to eliminate them. After fending off the squad, Horizon tells her squad she loves field research and rubs a leaf from Mirage's face and continues their match.
