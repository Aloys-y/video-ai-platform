## Treasure Pack Rewards
PlayingDuos,Trios, orRanked Leagues, players can findTreasure Packsin-game, once per day (players can also purchase all the available Treasure Packs for25 each).
Treasure Pack rewards vary with the only constant being that every four packs give out anEpicCharm.2 are given out usually after every other pack while15 or anApex Packare usually four packs apart.
All of the rewards need a total of 60Treasure Packsto unlock.
After all theTreasure Packsare collected, the player gets three rewards: theGold RushDevotion LMGSkin, anApex Pack, and theBFF GrenadeCharm.
