TheSyndicate Corps, formerly theFrontier Corps, is an interplanetary humanitarian agency.Lifelinewas a long-time volunteer before its hostile takeover.
## History
TheFrontier Corpswas founded in 2711 as a publicly funded volunteer program whose primary mission is to provide social, medical, and economic development support throughout the Frontier while promoting mutual understanding among citizens.[1]Prior to its reformation and rebranding in 2734, it was led by the President of the Frontier Corps.[2]Lifelinehad been using her winnings in theApex Gamesto help fund this organization.
After Torres Silva (under the guise of Eduardo Silva) was elected as the head of theMercenary Syndicatecouncil in 2734, he reformed the Frontier Corps into the Syndicate Corps, placingCherisse Cheat its head. After this point, the organization was fully funded by the Syndicate. Lifeline was listed as the spokesperson for the Syndicate Corps, likely unwillingly.[3]
