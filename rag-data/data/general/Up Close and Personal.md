Up Close and Personalis aStories from the Outlandsanimated short released on January 27, 2020, to promoteSeason 4.
## Synopsis
In an exclusive report from Outlands TV, field correspondentLisa Stoneinterviews five-time Hyperfighting Federation championJames "The Forge" McCormick; backed byHammond Robotics, the fighter has announced his intention to join theApex Games. Less than a minute into the interview,Revenantsneaks onto set and impales Forge from behind, killing him. The broadcast immediately cuts off.
## Details and easter eggs
- This interview was conducted in the train station at Sorting Factory onWorld's Edge, and prior to the release of the short, the set could be seen in-game. After the release, aDeath Boxwas added containing Forge's medallion; picking it up would add theSteel Cutweapon charmto the player's collection.
