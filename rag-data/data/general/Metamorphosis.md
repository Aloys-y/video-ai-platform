Metamorphosisis aStories from the Outlandsanimated short released on July 19, 2021, to promoteSeason 10.
## Synopsis
On the planetBoreas, citizens watch as a meteor strikes the moon Cleo with enough force to shatter half the surface. Meanwhile,Obi Edolasimis born, and the coincidence combined with his unusual, piercing blue eyes cause Obi to be considered a cursed child throughout his youth. Regardless, his parents love him greatly; his mother teaches him to dance and his father teaches him to use micro-drones. As an adult, Obi makes use of both talents in theArenas, and garners a large fanbase for his style, elegance, and pride.
