# Skydive Emote

Skydive Emotesare earnable cosmetics introduced inSeason 2that allowLegendsto do special moves whileskydiving.
Skydive Emotes can be earned in theBattle Pass, fromEvents, or purchased in theStore.
## Information
Once earned, they are automatically equipped and can be used by pressing the jump (Spacebar///) button while skydiving. If the player has multiple equipped, they can also hold the button to cycle through their current inventory of Skydive Emotes.
