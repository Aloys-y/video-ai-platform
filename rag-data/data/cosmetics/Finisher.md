# Finisher

Finishers(also known as Executions) are short animations that can be performed on downed enemies to finish them off, by pressing the interact/pickup button (default:E///).
Each finisher has their own duration, most of them taking around 4.5 – 6.5 seconds. When you are finishing someone, the target is invulnerable to any damage from other players orThe Ring, but you are vulnerable and cannot take any actions other than canceling the finisher. Taking gunfire or being downed will cancel the finisher. Successfully performing a finisher refills yourBody Shieldat the end.
Alternative finishers can be unlocked viaApex Packs, or purchased with1,200. Special finishers can also be used when Tier 3 of aPrestige Skinis equipped.
