# Prestige Skin

Prestige SkinsareMythictier evolutiveLegendskins, first added in the3rd Anniversary Event.
## Information
EachPrestige Skinhas 3 tiers. Upon unlocking Tier 1 of a Prestige Skin, a player will be able to complete challenges to unlock Tier 2 and Tier 3 at no additional cost. Players will have an unlimited amount of time to complete these challenges once available. Once a Prestige Skin Tier’s challenge is completed, the player will unlock that respective tier’s version of the Legend skin which can then be equipped. Players have the ability to equip any unlocked tier of a Prestige Skin once owned.
They are made available for purchase in theMythic Storefor 150 [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] Heirloom Shards, after their introductoryEventends.
