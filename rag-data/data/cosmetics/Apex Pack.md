# Apex Pack

Apex Packsareloot boxesthat provide random cosmetic items. There are various types of Packs:
- Apex Packs, which by default areRare, butEpicandLegendaryones may be offered in theStoreor duringEvents;
- Thematic Packs, which contain at least one item for theLegend,weapon, orcosmetictype they are assigned to;
- Collection Event Packs, which contain at least one item from theCollection Eventthey come from;
- Themed Event Packs, which contain at least one item from theThemed Eventthey come from.

## Probability
A few things to note:
- Apex Packs do not drop duplicates. If an item already owned is chosen to drop again, it'll be converted inCrafting Metals.[1]ACommonitem converts to15, aRareto30, anEpicto200, and aLegendaryto600.If the case ofCollection Event PacksandThemed Event Packs, if somehow all event items are owned before opening one, all the Pack contents will be replaced by an amount ofCoins equal to the Pack cost.
- There is no estimated chance forRareitems to drop, although they are logically rarer thanCommonitems.
- Rareitems not already in possession take priority overRareduplicates, which is whyRareCrafting Metals are harder to find compared to the other rarities sinceCommon,Epic, andLegendaryitems instead are fully random.
- ALegendaryitem is guaranteed to drop in the next Pack after 29 Packs are opened without finding any.[1]This only applies toRarePacks.
- [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]]Heirloom Shardsare guaranteed to drop in the next Pack after 499 Packs are opened without finding any, unless allMythicitems are already owned.[1]Collection Event PacksandThemed Event Packsdo not count towards this, and [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]]Heirloom Shardscannot be found in them.When [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]]Heirloom Shardsare found, the Pack is not consumed.[1]

## Obtaining
### Store
TheStorealways sellsRarePacks in their own "Apex Packs" section, costing100 each.LegendaryandThematicPacks may also be offered in the Store, often bundled with other items, otherwise they are offered as a standalone purchase for respectively500 and400 each.Collection Event Packscan be purchased for700 each during a Collection Event, or purchased from bundles.
### Experience
Up to 544RarePacks can be earned by leveling up.[1]They are spread out in the following way:
- Tier 1Level 2 - 20: one every level.Level 22 - 300: one every 2 levels.Level 300 - 500: one every 5 levels.
- Tiers 2-4Level 1-100: one every 10 levels.Level 100-200: one every 8 levels.Level 200-300: one every 6 levels.Level 300-400: one every 4 levels.Level 400-500: one every 2 levels.

## Trivia
- There is a rare Apex Pack animation causing the Pack to "blast off" after dropping all three items.
- Thematic PacksandCollection Event Packsproduce a different, metallic sound when being opened, compared to regular Packs.
- Players whose store region is set to Belgium cannot purchase Apex Packs due to a gambling law banning video game loot boxes. The ones earned through methods that don't involve purchasing them with real money (leveling up, challenges, Free Battle Pass rewards) can still be earned and opened, but the Packs cannot be bought withCoins, and the ones in the Premium Battle Pass are replaced withCrafting Metals:Rare: offers 60Crafting MetalsEpic: offers 250Crafting MetalsLegendary: offers 800Crafting Metals
- Monster Within Packsmistakenly had the same rarity drop rates asCollection Event Packsfor standard loot pool items when they were first released on October 12, 2021. They were fixed shortly after.
