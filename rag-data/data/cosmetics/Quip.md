# Quip

Quipsare special voice lines eachlegendsays during the Champion presentation or when they kill an enemy. They can also be equipped to the Quip Wheel.
## Types
### Intro Quips
Intro Quips are voice lines that the match's Champion says during the Champion presentation.
### Kill Quips
Kill Quips are voice lines players hear when they get killed. After performing a finisher on a player, you will also hear your own kill quip, but nearby enemies can hear it as well.
## Quip Wheel
The quip wheel can be activated by holding theH//button. This wheel contains up to 8 quips,Holo-Sprays, orEmotesthat players have unlocked and equipped. When playing your quips from this wheel, nearby enemies can hear them. It always has a "Celebration" quip at the top, which will trigger a voice line to congratulate their team on their good work. You can also bring up thePingwheel by scrolling left or right. When crouching 3 times in a short time or after killing an enemy, an option to either play a random quip, throw aHolo-Spray, or do anEmotewill pop up.
