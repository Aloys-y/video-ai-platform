# Cosmetic

Cosmeticsare items that can be acquired to further customize the player's experience. As the name suggests, these items are entirely cosmetic and do not grant any competitive advantage. They can be acquired through the use of various types ofcurrency.
## Types of cosmetics
### Skins
Skinschange the appearance of theLegendor theweaponthe skin has been assigned to. Up to eight skins can be equipped for each Legend and weapon, which cycle through every time the player connects to the Lobby.
- Commonskins present a monochromatic tone, with sections featuring different colors;
- Rareskins present a monochromatic tone, with sections featuring different textures;
- Epicskins present sections that are dynamic, or a more prominent change of colors and/or textures across the whole skin;[1]
- Legendary,IconicandMythicskins change, either partially or totally, the model of the Legend or the weapon. They may also present dynamic sections similarly toEpicskins along with the model change.
- Originaldefault skin for Wraith, for reference.
- OrchidCommon skin for Wraith.
- Scales of JusticeRare skin for Wraith.
- Sun BleachedEpic skin for Wraith.
- VoidwalkerLegendary skin for Wraith.
- Apex VoidshifterMythic skin for Wraith.
### Banners
Banner cosmetics change the appearance of the Banner card that appears after all the members of a squad have picked their Legend, when the Champion squad is shown, or during a match around the arena to showcase who the current Kill Leader or Champion, and their squad, are.
#### Banner Frames
Only oneBanner Framecan be equipped.
- CommonBanner Frames present a simple picture on the background;
- RareBanner Frames present a picture and a more adorned Banner;
- EpicBanner Frames expand the Banner boundaries and present more detailed themes and decorations;
- LegendaryandMythicBanner Frames present the same details asEpicBanner Frames, with the addition of dynamic animations.
- HivemindCommon Banner Frame for Revenant.
- HydraulicRare Banner Frame for Revenant.
- Stay On TargetEpic Banner Frame for Revenant.
- Element of SurpriseLegendary Banner Frame for Revenant. (Note that this Banner Frame is animated in-game.)
- Soul SacrificeMythic Banner Frame for Revenant. (Note that this Banner Frame is animated in-game.)
#### Banner Poses
Only oneBanner Posecan be equipped.
- RareBanner Poses change the pose of the Legend into a different, static one;
- EpicandMythicBanner Poses change the pose of the Legend into a dynamic one.
LegendaryBanner Poses do not exist.CommonBanner Poses are the default ones.
- Peacedefault Banner Pose for Lifeline.
- BFFRare Banner Pose for Lifeline.
- FunslingerEpic Banner Pose for Lifeline. Note that this Pose is animated in-game.
- Shock SticksMythic Banner Pose for Lifeline. Note that this Pose is animated in-game.
#### Stat Trackers
Up to threeStat Trackerscan be equipped on a Banner.
- CommonTrackers track generic stats such as Kills or Damage Done;
- RareTrackers track either Legend-specificAbility-related or Seasonal stats, or feature concept art of a Legend skin.
#### Badges
Up to threeBadgescan be equipped on a Banner.
### Finishers
Finisherschange the way a Legend can kill an enemy while the latter isBleeding Out. Only one Finisher can be equipped at any given time.
Besides theCommonFinishers which are the default ones, Finishers exist inLegendaryandMythicrarity.MythicFinishers can only be performed when a Legend'sPrestige Skinis equipped, either any Tier or Tier 3 only depending on the Legend.

### Skydive Emotes
Skydive Emotesare short animations a Legend can perform, either to show off to squadmates or to taunt enemies, during a match. However, they can only be played while dropping into the arena, or after taking aJump Tower. They can be equipped to their own Skydive Emote Wheel, and up to eight of them can be equipped at the same time.
Skydive Emotes only exist inEpicrarity.

- https://apexlegends.fandom.com/wiki/File:SkydiveEmote_Mirage_It_Takes_Two.mp4It Takes TwoSkydive Emote for Mirage.
### Emotes
Similarly to Skydive Emotes,Emotesare short animations a Legend can perform, either to show off to squadmates or to taunt enemies, during a match. They can be equipped to the Emote Wheel, and up to eight across Emotes, Holosprays and Quips can be equipped at the same time.
- RareEmotes are the default ones;
- EpicEmotes are simple animations and serve as an alternative toRareones;
- LegendaryEmotes present either more complex animations, or the Legend interacting with their abilities in entertaining ways.
- MythicEmotes have different variations each time the Emote is performed.
### Holosprays
Holospraysare holographic images that can be placed by Legends around the arena. Up to two, or three when not playing inBattle Royale, Holosprays can be placed. They can be equipped to the Emote Wheel, and up to eight across Emotes, Holosprays and Quips can be equipped at the same time.
While most Holosprays can only be equipped by the Legend being depicted in it, some Event or limited-time Holosprays can be equipped by all Legends.
Holosprays exist inRare,EpicandLegendaryrarities. Although there is no effective difference betweenRareandEpicHolosprays,Legendaryones present dynamic animations across the image.
- ALGS Playoffs '21Rare Holospray for all Legends.
- Nyah!Epic Holospray for Wattson.
- Fire StarterHolospray for Mad Maggie. Note that this Holospray is animated in-game.
### Weapon Charms
Weapon Charmsare small trinkets that can be attached to a weapon. Only one Charm can be assigned to each weapon, and the same Charm cannot be attached to multiple weapons.
Charms exist inRare,EpicandLegendaryrarities, although there is no effective difference between them.
- Nox's TrapRare Weapon Charm.
- Beach BumEpic Weapon Charm.
- Bobblehead RevenantLegendary Weapon Charm.
### Skydive Trails
Skydive Trailsare dynamic effects that replace the random colored smoke trail that appears when dropping into the arena. They could formerly earned as seasonal rewards fromRankedfor reachingDiamondor above, but now can only be obtained as part ofPrestige Skinsets.  Only one Skydive Trail can be equipped at any given time.
Except forSeason 2andSeason 3trails which are unlocked permanently, ranked reward Skydive Trails lasted only for the duration of a Season.
### Quips
Quipsare voicelines that can be equipped to a Legend, which are played depending on the situation. While only one Intro Quip and Kill Quip can be equipped for their intended purpose, they can also be equipped to the Emote Wheel, and up to eight across Emotes, Holosprays and Quips can be equipped at the same time.
##### Intro Quips
Intro Quipsare played when a Legend is introduced as the Champion. They exist inCommon,RareandMythicrarities, with theRareones also playing a background sound, usually related to the Legend abilities.
##### Kill Quips
When a Legend kills an opponent, aKill Quipis played to the opponent. Kill Quips are also played out loud after finishing an opponent. They exist inCommon,RareandMythicrarities, although there is no difference between them.
### Transitions
Transitions, previously namedLoading Screens, are background images that appear when loading into a match.
Transitions exist inCommonandRarerarities, although there is no effective difference between them.
### Music Packs
Music Packsare music tracks that play while in the Lobby or while dropping into a match.
Music Packs only exist inEpicrarity.
### Stickers
Stickersare small images that can be placed on certainRegenitems: Health Injectors (SyringesandMed Kits),Shield Cells,Shield BatteriesandPhoenix Kits. Up to three stickers (limited to two onSyringesandShield Cells) can be placed on each item, but the same sticker cannot be applied to multiple items at the same time.
## Acquiring cosmetics
Cosmetics can be acquired in various ways.
### Crafting
By utilizingCrafting Metals, a player can directly obtain a cosmetic of choice across the permanently available items found in the Legends and Loadout sections of the Lobby. The cost of the cosmetics vary depending on their rarity:
- Commonitems cost30;
- Rareitems cost60;
- Epicitems cost400;
- Legendaryitems cost1,200.
### Apex Packs
By openingApex Packs, a player can obtain three random cosmetic items across the permanently available items found in the Legends and Loadout sections of the Lobby. While the type of cosmetic is also random, rarity plays a role:
- At least oneRareor better item is guaranteed to drop;
- AnEpicor better item has a 24.8% chance to drop;
- ALegendaryitem has a 7.4% chance to drop.
Apex Packs cost100 each.
There are also various types of Packs that can guarantee an item from either a certain rarity, a certain Legend, or a certain cosmetic type.
### Store
By heading to theStore, a player can directly obtain a cosmetic between the ones currently offered by exchanging their currency:
- Rareitems cost500;
- Epicitems cost1,000;
- Legendaryitems cost1,800;
- Legendaryrecolors cost1,800, or either6,500 or10,500.
The Store may also offer Bundles which contain multiple items of various types. Prices for Bundles vary depending on their contents.
### Battle Pass
EachSeason, aBattle Passis offered for950. Battle Passes contain a wide variety of cosmetics, Packs, and currency.
Battle Pass items introduced fromSeason 11onwards may get offered in the Store after at least two Seasons.
### Ranked Rewards
Badges, charms, banner frames and holosprays can be earned as rewards for reaching certain ranks in each season.
Badges and holosprays are rewards forBronzeand above, charms are rewards forGoldand above, and banner frames are rewards forDiamondand above.
### Events
#### Collection Events
Collection Eventsintroduce 24 limited-time cosmetics (12Legendary, 12Epic) into the game. For two weeks these cosmetics can be directly acquired by exchanging currency:
- Epicitems cost1,000 or800;
- Legendaryitems cost1,800 or2,400.
- Mythicitems are only obtainable when a player purchases all 24 collection event cosmetics. They are permanently available in theMythicItem Shop after the event.
If the player instead wishes to try their luck,Collection Event Packsare offered for700 each, which are guaranteed to drop a Collection Event item every time a Pack is opened, albeit the item being random.
After the Event ends, these items cannot be crafted anymore, and can only be acquired whenever the Store offers them after at least two Seasons have passed.
#### Thematic Events
Thematic Eventsintroduce 40 limited-time cosmetics (8Legendary, 16Epic, 16Rare) into the game.
- Rareitems cost500 or120;
- Epicitems cost1,000 or800;
- Legendaryitems cost1,800 or2,400.
If the player instead wishes to try their luck,Thematic Event Packsare offered for400 each, which are guaranteed to drop a Thematic Event item every time a Pack is opened, albeit the item being random.
After the Event ends, these items are added into the permanent loot pool, which means they can be found inApex Packsor crafted, while still keeping the increased crafting cost.
After at least two Seasons have passed, the price for Thematic Event items gets halved, matching the price of the other permanent items.
#### Prize Track
During an Event aPrize Trackoften also unlocks, allowing players to earn free rewards by completingChallenges.
## Mythic cosmetics
While most cosmetics items can be found relatively easily,Mythicitems are hard to obtain.
### Heirloom Shards
Very rarely, after opening certain types ofApex Packs, [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]]Heirloom Shardsmay get dropped. These Shards allow the player to acquire aMythicitem of choice through theMythic Storefor [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150 each. An unlimited amount of Heirloom Shards can be found from Packs as long as the player doesn't currently own all available Mythic items.
### Heirlooms
Heirloom Setsare a set of three cosmetic items for a Legend: a Melee Weapon Skin, a Banner Pose, and generally an Intro Quip (the exceptions beingPathfinder's set andWraith'sHope's Dawnset). The Melee Weapon Skin replaces the typical melee animations with unique ones featuring the Melee Weapon. The Banner Pose is animated, and presents the Legend and their Melee Weapon.
Heirloom Sets may be acquired by purchasing every item from a Collection Event, after which the player is awarded with the newly introduced Set. After the Event ends, the Heirloom Set is added in the Mythic Store and can be purchased anytime, as long as the player has at least [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150.
### Prestige Skins
Prestige Skinsare Legend skins that come with a themed Finisher and (with the exception of a few) a Skydive Trail. Prestige Skins possess three different "Tiers": Tier 1 is unlocked instantly, while Tier 2 and Tier 3 require the player to deal 30,000 and 100,000 damage respectively with the Legend to unlock.
Prestige Skins may be acquired by purchasing every item from a Thematic or Collection Event, after which the player is awarded with the newly introduced Skin. After the Event ends, the Prestige Skin set is added in the Mythic Store and can be purchased anytime for [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] 150.
