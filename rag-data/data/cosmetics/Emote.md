# Emote

| This article is about emotes used on the ground. For emotes used while skydiving, seeSkydive Emote.
Emotesarecosmeticsintroduced inSeason 9that allow the player to do special moves, dances, and taunts while on the ground.
## About
- Emotes are viewed in third-person but have a unique “anti-peek” feature, which prevents players from being able to see enemies behind corners not normally visible in the first-person view. This is disabled in theFiring Range.
- TheLegend's hitbox still follows their model, meaning that it is possible to dodge bullets with an emote.
- Each legend has one free emote.
- Can also be done on the victory screen, either a random emote or an emote chosen manually from the emote wheel.
- Can be favorited from the cosmetics tab, at which point crouch-spam prompts and victory screen prompts will prioritize favorited emotes over others selected in the emote wheel. If more than one is favorited, one will play randomly from the favorites pool.
- Cannot be used if you are too close to a wall.
