# Store

The Store as ofSeason 12.
TheStoreis a menu accessible through the lobby wherecosmeticsitems can be bought usingApex Coins,Legend Tokens, orHeirloom Shards.
While the Store normally offers an alternative purchase method withApex Coins for items that can either be crafted withCrafting Metals or be found inApex Packs, sinceSeason 10Collection Event and Thematic Event items may also appear, along with Store exclusive items that are not tied to any event.
## Specials
In the past, a Store Takeover would've happened as a result of a minor event, where items tied to such event would replace the standard structure of the "Featured" section of the Store. SinceSeason 11, instead of a Store Takeover happening, aSpecialssection opens, thus allowing the Featured section to cycle through normally.
However, during some Store sales such as Recolor Stores, the "Featured" section may still be overridden to display only the cosmetics tied to the sale.
Starting withSeason 14, aMonthly Storewas added, featuring rotation monthly specials.
## Featured
The Store's main section, calledFeatured, is split into two subcategories:FeaturedandLimited-Time. The Featured section updates weekly at 02:00:00 UTC (GMT+0), while the Limited-Time section updates twice per week.
The Featured section features four slots that can be occupied byLegendarySkins,Weapon Charms,Holosprays, or bundles. The cost of the bundles depends on which and how many items the bundle contains, with an added discount that is adjusted accordingly if one or more items from the bundle are already in possession. Bundles that offer a Skin plusApex Packsare made unavailable if the Skin is already owned. The Limited-Time category offers twoLegendarySkins (that are otherwise craftable) costing1,800 each, plus a recolor for each of the two Skins which can be purchased with10,500 or1,800 as long as the original item is owned.

## Mythic Store
TheMythic Storeallows the purchasing ofHeirloom SetsandPrestige Skins.
Heirloom Sets are bundles of three cosmetic items that contain a Melee Weapon Skin, an Intro or Kill Quip, and a Banner Pose.
Prestige Skins areMythic-quality Legend Skins which can be "evolved" through challenges whose tracking can be toggled on/off, and can also be completed at any time. Unlocking the final tier of a Prestige Skin will also unlock aFinisherwhich can only be performed when that skin is equipped.
Items offered in the Mythic Store cost 150 [[File:{{{2}}} Shards.svg|25px|{{{2}}} Shards|link={{{2}}}Shards]] Heirloom Shards.

## Trivia
- The first recolors ever released in the Store wereTotal EclipseandThe Blue Dragon, for theProwler Burst PDWandG7 Scoutrespectively, on February 13, 2019.Therefore, the Prowler and G7 are the weapons that got a recolor in the shortest amount of time, after just 9 days.
- Wraithis the Legend that got a recolor in the shortest amount of time:Airship Assassinon February 23, 2019, after 19 days.
- Octaneis the Legend released after the game's launch that got a recolor in the least amount of time:Red Shifton April 20, 2019, after 32 days.
- Lobais the Legend that got a recolor after the highest amount of time:Rum Runneron July 27, 2021, after 441 days.
- Perfect IllusionMirageis currently the Legend recolor that took the highest amount of time to be released: present in the game's files since the game's launch, it was released on August 24, 2021, after 932 days.
- Focus FireMozambique Shotgunis currently the weapon recolor that took the highest amount of time to be released: present in the game's files sinceSeason 3, it was released on May 17, 2022, after 959 days.
- Mirage and theDevotion LMGcurrently have the highest amount of recolors, tied at six.
- WhenOcean SprayAlternator SMGandPole PositionPathfinderwere first released, they could've been acquired without owning their required skin, effectively making them free. In both cases, this was fixed shortly after.
- Rarely, when a recolor comes back, it might not require the same skin it used to when it first got released. It's not clear if it's intended or a mistake, due toRespawnnot always fixing the requirements if this happens. Also, some recolors seem to have their requirements swapped:Holeshot HotshotOctane looking similar toThe Victory Laprather thanGold Rush, its required skin;Masked StorytellerCryptohaving the same mask design asRising Phoenixrather thanThe Masked Dancer, its required skin;
- The Blue DragonG7 lacks the recolor symbol in the Loadout.
- Folk HeroMirage is currently the rarest recolor to date, being last available on February 8, 2020 and has never made a return since then.
