# Twitch Drop

Twitch Dropsare rewards that are given to players after fulfilling certain conditions, usually having a certain amount of watch time, on a Twitch stream.
Twitch Drops rewards forApex Legendswere first introduced on March 27, 2021 during the Apex Legends Global Series Winter Circuit Playoffs.
