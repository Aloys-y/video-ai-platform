# Locked and Loaded (LTT)

Locked and Loadedis aLimited Time Takeoverintroduced inSeason 8'sAnniversary Collection Event.
Due to being a Takeover, Locked and Loaded directly affects thePlay Apexgame mode rather than being in a separate mode selection.
