# TDM - Unshielded

TDM - Unshielded(formerly known as TDM-Deadeye or TDM Unshielded Deadeye) is aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 16'sVeiled Collection Event.
## Availability
- April 25, 2023 - May 9, 2023
- May 23, 2023 - June 6, 2023(replacedControlin theMixtape playlist)
## Details
The gameplay is the same asTeam Deathmatch, except:
- Legends have noHelmets,Body Shields, orGrenades.
- There are no neutralCare Packages.
- All legends have the same loadout which is chosen randomly each match. Possible loadouts include:Wingmanand30-30 RepeaterHemlok Burst ARandG7 ScoutKraber .50-Cal SniperandSentinelTheKraber .50-Cal Sniper's ammo is unlimited.
- Lifeline'sCare Packagecontains threeBody Shields.
## Maps
- Overflow
- Skull Town
- Habitat 4
