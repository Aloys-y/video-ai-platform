# Ultra Zones

Ultra Zonesis aLimited Time Takeoverintroduced inSeason 8'sWar Games Themed Event.
Due to being a Takeover, Ultra Zones directly affects thePlay Apexgame mode rather than being in a separate mode selection.
## Availability
- April 15, 2021 - April 19, 2021
- April 19, 2021 - April 21, 2021
## Details
Instead of one Hot Zone, three Hot Zones appear on the map. Each Hot Zone is engulfed in aFlashpoint Zone.
