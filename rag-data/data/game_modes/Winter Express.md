# Winter Express

Winter Expressis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 3'sHolo-Day Bash Collection Event.
In this game mode, theAnnouncerisMirage.
## Availability
- December 12, 2019 - January 7, 2020
- December 1, 2020 - January 4, 2021
- December 7, 2021 - December 28, 2021
- December 6, 2022 - December 27, 2022
## Details
### Players
- Three teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
- A Legend's damaged  Body Shield automatically starts regenerating after not taking damage for 4 seconds. The cooldown is doubled to 8 seconds if the shield got fully depleted instead.
- All Legends are pre-equipped with aBody Shield, aHelmet, infiniteShield Cells, infiniteSyringes, and infiniteMed Kits. They can also respawn after each Round.
- Legends cannot get knocked down and enter theBleeding Outstate, dying instead as soon as theirhealthis depleted. When a Legend dies, they drop 40 ammo that varies depending on who scored the kill and theirordnance, if any.
- Ash'sMarked for Deathwill automatically ping a squad member's killer upon their death, due to the lack ofDeath Boxes.
- Lifeline'sCare Packagealways contains:aBody Shield;aShield BatteryorMed Kit, which can be used endlessly;3 randomShield CellsandSyringes.
### Gameplay
The goal of Winter Express is capturing the Train or killing everyone.
- The Train stops at a Station each Round.The available Stations in order are: Capitol City, Fool's Gulch, Refinery, Derailment, and the area outside the tunnel located after Derailment.
- The whole Train functions as a control point, which must be boarded to start capturing it. Capturing the Train, or killing every enemy player so that no one can contest the capture, yields 1 point. Once a point is scored, the Round ends and a new one begins.Capturing the Train takes 10 seconds, if uncontested.
- Whenever a Round starts, a team spawns on the Train while the other two teams spawn on a Dropship near the Station.Players launching from the Dropship will start skydiving.In the first Round, the Champions spawn on the Train.In the subsequent Rounds, the latest team that scored a point spawns on the Train. Dead teammates are also respawned there.
- Players can choose between five different weapon Loadouts, which vary according to the Loadout's name. All Loadouts will also have one type ofordnance.All weapons areFully Kittedand range betweenCommon,Rare, andEpic, depending on the Loadout. Optics can be removed and/or replaced, but not in the Loadout selection.Loadouts can be changed at any point during the match, but only while the player is awaiting respawn.The available Loadouts cycle every 15 minutes, but only after a new match is started.
- A Round lasts 90 seconds.The countdown starts when the Train stops at a Station.If a point isn't awarded after 60 seconds, Overtime begins, where only the middle section of the Train functions as a control point instead of the whole Train.If a point isn't awarded after 90 seconds, the Round ends in a tie and a new one begins.
- Scattered on the Train rails are some launching platforms, which send whoever crosses them into the air, allowing them to skydive to a location of choice.
- Unlimited Weapon Ammo.

### Winning
- The first team that scores 3 points wins.
