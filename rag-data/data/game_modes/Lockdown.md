# Lockdown

Lockdown

Permanent Mode
First introduced | March 26, 2024
Lockdownis aMixtapegame modein which four teams compete to take control of temporary zones. It was introduced as aLimited Time ModeinSeason 20'sShadow Societyevent, then permanently added through the Mixtape playlist inSeason 21.
## Details
### Players
- Four teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.The teams are split into Wolf Squad, Eagle Squad, Bear Squad, and Tiger Squad. They are respectively colored, blue, purple, pink, and orange.
- All Legends are pre-equipped with aBody Shield, aHelmet, infinite ammo, infiniteShield Cells, and infiniteShield Batteries.
- Healthwill regenerate automatically after not taking damage for 6 seconds.
- Legends cannot get knocked down and enter theBleeding Outstate, dying instead as soon as their health is depleted. When a Legend dies, they drop theirweaponsandordnance.
- Five weapon loadouts are available to select from, and can change while awaiting respawn.Weapon optics can be changed while selecting a loadout.
- Ash'sMarked for Deathwill automatically ping a squad member's killer upon their death, due to the lack ofDeath Boxes.
- Lifeline'sCare Packagealways contains threeBody Shields.
### Gameplay
The goal of Lockdown is to score points by capturing and holding Capture Zones.
- Small Zones periodically open on carious points of the map, however they have a 30 second cooldown before they can start being captured.When a Zone becomes available for capture, it is marked as "Neutral".When a Zone becomes available for capture, it remains open for 1 minute and 30 seconds. After which, it closes and a new one immediately opens somewhere else.A Zone may stay open more than usual if a Last Stand is happening on it, with at least two Legends of different teams being on it while the Zone has technically expired.
- On match start, Zone A opens on the map. After Zone A has been opened for a minute (30 second cooldown, plus 30 seconds available for capture), Zone B opens on the map.
- When a Zone is captured, the alive members of the team who captured it get fully healed if they were on it, and at the center of the Zone, 3ordnance, 2Fully Kitted Weapon, and aSupply Drop Weaponare spawned.
- Once a team reaches 400 points, the Zone behavior changes slightly: with only one Zone being available at a time, rather than two.The first time this occurs, once the Zone nearest to closure is captured or expires, it will not relocate until the remaining Zone is also cleared.
- Points are awarded based on the following actions: Holding Zone (every 3 seconds) and Enemy kill, each awarding 5 points.Contested Zones still award "Holding Zone" points to all teams that are on it.During a Last Stand, no "Holding Zone" points are awarded.
### Winning
There are two ways to win in Lockdown
- The first team to reach 400 points wins.
- Lockdown matches have a time limit of 10 minutes. If none of the teams have reached 400 points when the time expires, the team with the most points wins.
