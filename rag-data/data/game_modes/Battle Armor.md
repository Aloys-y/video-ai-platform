# Battle Armor

Battle Armoris aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 4.
This modeEscalates, meaning it levels up and changes after a few days.
## Availability
- April 28, 2020 - May 12, 2020
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
- All Legends are pre-equipped with aP2020equipped with a1x Holo, 20Light Rounds, and 2Syringes. They are also pre-equipped with aBody Shieldthat changes depending on the level of the mode:During Level 1, Legends are pre-equipped with aBody Shield;During Level 2, Legends are pre-equipped with aBody Shield;During Level 3, Legends are pre-equipped with aBody Shield;During Level 4, Legends are pre-equipped with anEvo Shield.
### Gameplay
The goal of Battle Armor is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asPlay Apex.
- Body Shieldscannot be found.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
## Maps
The only available map for Battle Armor isWorld's Edge.
