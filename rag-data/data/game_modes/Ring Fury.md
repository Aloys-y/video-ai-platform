# Ring Fury

Ring Furyis aLimited Time Takeoverintroduced inSeason 8'sChaos Theory Collection Event.
Due to being a Takeover, Ring Fury directly affects thePlay Apexgame mode rather than being in a separate mode selection.
This modeEscalates, meaning it levels up and changes after a few days.
During this Takeover, theAnnouncerisMad Maggie.
## Availability
- March 9, 2021 - March 23, 2021
## Details
In addition toThe Ring, whenever a Round starts, multiple Ring Flares appear on the map. Ring Flares act as smaller Rings, which also deal the same damage as the Ring depending on the current Round, but it damages Legends caught inside them, unlike The Ring which damages Legends outside it.
The amount and size of Ring Flares vary depending on the level of the Takeover:
- Level 1 has a small number of small Ring Flares across the map;
- Level 2 has a large Ring Flare inside the Ring, with small Ring Flares across the map;
- Level 3 has a small number of large Ring Flares inside the Ring, with small Ring Flares across the map;
- Level 4 has a high number of large Ring Flares across the map;
- Level 5 has at least 20 Ring Flares of various size active on the map at the same time.
