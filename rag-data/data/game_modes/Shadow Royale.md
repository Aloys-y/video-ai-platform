# Shadow Royale

Shadow Royaleis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 6'sFight or Fright Themed Event.
In this game mode, theAnnouncerisShadow Revenant.
## Availability
- October 22, 2020 - November 3, 2020
- October 28, 2021 - November 2, 2021
- October 4, 2022 - October 11, 2022
- October 25, 2022 - November 1, 2022
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
- Legends cannot be respawned.
### Gameplay
The goal of Shadow Royale is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asPlay Apex.
- If a Legend dies, they become a Shadow.Shadows have 65health, a melee attack that deals 50 damage per hit and instantly destroys doors, increased movement speed, and can wallrun and double jump. They also cannot loot anything, can reviveBleeding Outteammates, and can performFinisherson Bleeding Out enemies to fully heal themselves.Shadows emit a constant loud, eerie noise, which can be heard to acknowledge their position even when they're standing still.Shadows keep respawning automatically after dying as long as there's at least one member of the squad who isn't a Shadow. Once the last non-Shadow member dies, the squad is eliminated.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
