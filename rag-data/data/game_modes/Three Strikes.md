# Three Strikes

Three Strikesis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 19'sApex Legends X Post MaloneEvent.
In this game mode, theAnnouncerisOctane.
## Availability
- November 7, 2023 - November 21, 2023
- November 23, 2023 - November 28, 2023
- January 31, 2024 - February 13, 2024
## Details
- After a squad is killed, they will respawn while skydiving one POI away with all items and Evo points they had upon death (exceptSupply Drop Weapons).Squads can respawn a maximum of two times.Respawns are disabled when the finalRingcountdown begins
- Knocked players cannot die unless all teammates are knocked or they fall off the map, but they can choose to manually bleed out.
- Revives take 1 second, and players are revived with 70 Health compared to the usual 20.
- Ammo stacks will spawn next to a squad'sDeath Boxesupon death.
