# Second Chance

Second Chanceis aLimited Time Takeoverintroduced inSeason 8'sWar Games Themed Event.
Due to being a Takeover, Second Chance directly affects thePlay Apexgame mode rather than being in a separate mode selection.
## Availability
- April 13, 2021
## Details
AllLegendsare pre-equipped with one Respawn Token. When a Legend dies for their first time, they drop oneShield Cell, oneSyringe, and a stack of ammo for the enemy's weapon that killed them; they also automatically use their Respawn Token, respawning directly above the location where they died with all their items. Successive deaths act as usual. The Respawn Token may also be used manually whenBleeding Out.

- War Games:Kings Canyon,Olympus
