# Revenant Uprising

Revenant Uprisingis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 19'sUprisingEvent.
## Availability
- December 5, 2023 - January 2, 2024
## Details
### Gameplay
- At the beginning of the match, 30 players will join the Legends team, while the other 30 join the Revenant Army.
- An Evac Point is marked on the map, and activates after 8 minutes. Any surviving Legend that reaches the Evac Point when it activates will escape, and if enough do so, the Legends will win.
- If the required amount of Legends don't Evacuate, the Revenant Army wins.
- All weapons spawn Fully Kitted, andAmmois infinite.As such, ammo andAttachments(exceptOptics) do not spawn.
- Nearby enemies are highlighted orange.
### Legend team
- Legends spawn with aP2020,Shield BatteryandMed Kit, on top of the normal BR loadout.
- Legends can revive any other Legend, even if not in their squad, and Legends are revived with full health.
- If a Legend is killed but at least one squadmate survives, they can be respawned at aRespawn Beacon, along with all other deceased Legends.
- If a squad is fully eliminated, they will join the Revenant Army. In the final 2 minutes, this is disabled, and eliminated squads will spectate the surviving Legends.
### Revenant Army
- Revenant Army members have enhanced speed, the ability to wall-run and double jump, a long-range melee attack that deals 50 damage, and aShadow Pouncecooldown of 8 seconds.However, they cannot equip or use any items or gear, nor use anyMap Featuresaside fromZiplinesand big doors. They also cannot useForged Shadows.
- Revenant Army members will skydive from the Revenant Army's Dropship when the round starts. Afterwards, they respawn at ground some distance from the survivors.
- Their health will gradually recover after they don't take any damage for a while.
- Every time Revenant Army member knocks down or eliminate a survivor, his health will replenish to full.
- Their map will periodically reveal the location of all surviving Legends.
- If a Revenant Army member is killed, they respawn after 6 seconds.
- Dealing the most damage as a Revenant Army member allows the player to respawn as a Red Eyed Revenant or press Ultimate Button when prompted to be chosen to reborn as Red Eyed Revenant.Only One Red Eyed Revenant activates.The Red Eyed Revenant always skydives every times he spawns.The Red Eyed Revenant spawns with anEvo Shield,Helmet,Knockdown Shield, aProwler Burst PDWandKraber .50-Cal Sniperwith infinite ammo, aPhoenix Kit, fourShield Batteries, fourMed Kits, twoSyringesand twoShield Cells, anArc Star, anFrag Grenade, anThermite Grenadeand anEvac Tower.Every time the Red Eyed Revenant eliminates a survivor, he gets his Body Shield replenished to full.He does not have the enhanced movement, attacks or tactical cooldown of the Revenant Army, but he can equip and use items andMap Featureslike the Legend team, and can useForged Shadows.The Red Eyed Revenant's position is permanently marked to everyone.
