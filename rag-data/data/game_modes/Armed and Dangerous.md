# Armed and Dangerous

Armed and Dangerous, also calledArmed & Dangerous, is aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 2'sVoidwalker Themed Event.
## Availability
- June 20, 2023 - July 4, 2023
- July 19, 2022 - August 2, 2022
- September 15, 2020 - September 22, 2020
- June 23, 2020 - July 7, 2020
- January 22, 2020 - January 23, 2020
- September 3, 2019 - September 17, 2019
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of Armed and Dangerous is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asBattle Royale.
- Only weapons categorized asShotgunsorSniperscan spawn.
- Onlyattachmentscompatible with the available weapons can spawn.
- Loot sources that aren'tSupply Binsspawn items less frequently.
- Weapons andSyringesspawn more frequently.
- Fully Kittedversions of the available weapons may spawn inHigh Tierloot zones.
- If there areSupply DropShotguns or Snipers inBattle Royale, these may also rarely spawn as ground loot in Armed and Dangerous.
- Evo Shields,Body Shields, andHelmetscannot spawn.
Depending on when Armed and Dangerous was available, there were some additional changes.
Dressed to Kill
- Rather than spawning in place of other weapons,Supply Drop Weaponsmay rarely be found instead as bonus loot insideSupply Bins, popping up similarly toTreasure Packs.
- TheCharge Riflecannot be found as ground loot. However, aFully Kittedone may be crafted in aReplicatorfor100.
- Level 3Helmetsmay be crafted in aReplicatorfor30when available.
Gaiden
- Supply Drop Weaponscan only be found in Care Packages. However, during Saturday and Sunday, they (KraberandMastiff) may be crafted in aReplicatorfor150each as well.
September Soirée
- Level 1HelmetsandEvo Shieldsspawn less frequently. TheStarter Kitwas not available at this time.
Lost Treasures
- Name changed toArmed and Dangerous Evolved.
- Respawn Beaconscannot be found. The only way to respawn Legends are throughMobile Respawn Beacons.
- Level 1Body Shieldscannot be found.
- Level 1HelmetsandEvo Shieldsspawn less frequently. TheStarter Kitwas not available at this time.
- Changed ammo values:Shotgun Shells: 7 on pickup, 28 stack sizeSniper Ammo: 7 on pickup, 14 stack size.

and
Voidwalker
- Shield Batteriesspawn less frequently.
- Level 1HelmetsandBody Shieldsspawn less frequently. TheStarter Kitwas not available at this time.

### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
