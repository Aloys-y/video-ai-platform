# Gold Rush Duos

Gold Rush Duosis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 3'sGrand Soirée Themed Event.
## Availability
- January 14, 2020 - January 16, 2020
## Details
### Players
- Thirty teams of twoLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of Gold Rush Duos is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asBattle Royale.
- The only available weapons are seasonalFully Kittedones andSupply Dropones.
- Supply Drop Weaponscan be found as ground loot.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
