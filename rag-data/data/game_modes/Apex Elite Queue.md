# Apex Elite Queue

Apex Elite Queueis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 1'sLegendary Hunt Themed Event.
## Availability
- June 4, 2019 - July 2, 2019
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of Apex Elite Queue is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asBattle Royale.
- The Ringcloses faster.
- Out of Bounds damage is increased.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
- This mode remains accessible as long as a Top 5 is reached in every match.
