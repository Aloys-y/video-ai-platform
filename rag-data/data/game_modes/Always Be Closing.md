# Always Be Closing

Always Be Closingis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 3'sGrand Soirée Themed Event.
## Availability
- August 11, 2020 - August 18, 2020
- January 20, 2020 - January 21, 2020
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of Always Be Closing is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asBattle Royale.
- The Ringnever stops closing until the Final Round, where it then acts normally, and always deals Final Round damage.
Depending on when Always Be Closing was available, there were some additional changes.
Season 5
- Name changed toAlways Be Closing Evolved.
- All Legends are pre-equipped with anEvo Shield, aMozambique, and 16Shotgun Shells.
- TheGraffiti ModHop-Up can be found as ground loot.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
