# Flashpoint (LTM)

Flashpointis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 6'sAftermarket Collection Event.
## Availability
- October 6, 2020 - October 20, 2020
- April 19, 2022 - May 3, 2022
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of Flashpoint is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asPlay Apex.
- Some named locations on the map will be engulfed in a Flashpoint Zone, which will automatically restore a Legend's damagedshieldsorhealthvalues after not taking damage for 6 seconds.The locations are randomized each match.
- Shield Cells,Syringes,Shield Batteries,Med Kits, andPhoenix Kitscannot be found.Shield Batteries,Med Kits, andPhoenix Kitsmay still be crafted at aReplicator.
- The Ringnever stops closing until the Final Round, where it then acts normally, and always deals Final Round damage.The Final Round will always happen where a Flashpoint Zone is active.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
## Maps
- Unshackled:Olympus
- Aftermarket:Kings Canyon
