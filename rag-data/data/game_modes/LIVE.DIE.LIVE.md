# LIVE.DIE.LIVE

LIVE.DIE.LIVEis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 3'sGrand Soirée Themed Event.
## Availability
- September 29, 2020 - October 6, 2020
- January 16, 2020 - January 18, 2020
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
- When a Legend dies, they don't drop their Banner Card.
### Gameplay
The goal of LIVE.DIE.LIVE is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asBattle Royale.
- Dead members of a team are automatically respawned directly above their teammates wheneverThe Ringcloses and a new Round starts, as long as there's at least one alive member from their team.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
