# DUMMIEs Big Day

DUMMIEs Big Dayis aLimited Time Modeintroduced inSeason 3'sGrand Soirée Themed Event.
## Availability
- January 26, 2020 - January 28, 2020
- September 8, 2020
- September 22, 2020 - September 29, 2020
## Details
### Players
- Twenty teams of threeLegendscompete against each other.
### Gameplay
The goal of DUMMIEs Big Day is surviving, killing enemy players if necessary.
- Gameplay is the same asPlay Apex.
- The only available Legend isDUMMIE.There are eight colors for DUMMIEs to choose from: blue, orange, green, purple, red, grey, pink, and yellow.
### Winning
- The last team that survives wins.
## Maps
The only available map for DUMMIEs Big Day isWorld's Edge.
