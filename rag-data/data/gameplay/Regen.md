# Regen

This page lists all of theRegenitems currently in the game.
## Health Kits
These items can be accessed by pressing4orwhen shields or health are low. While consuming these items, players' movements are slowed, they cannot hang on edges, run, slide, or use any other item, but the consumption can be canceled any time.
As part of theStarter Kitfor Battle Royale, eachLegenddrops with 4Syringesand 4Shield Cellsand aLevel 1Knockdown Shield.

## Regen Abilities
Somelegendshaveabilitieswhich give them new regen options, or augment existing ones. For further information, see the respective legends' pages.
Legend | Ability | Description
Lifeline | D.O.C. Heal Drone | Deploys a drone which automatically attaches to Lifeline and her teammates if they are in its range. Rapidly regenerates health and disconnects if taking damage.
Octane | Swift Mend | Octane's health regenerates by 1 HP per second. Will stop if taking damage and reactivate after 6 seconds of not taking damage.
Revenant | Forged Shadows | While active, forms a 75 HP Shadow Shroud around Revenant that lasts 20 seconds, and regenerates slowly over time. Knocks, kills, and assists add an extra 5 seconds to Forged Shadows' duration.
Conduit | Radiant Transfer | Provides temporary shields to Conduit and a nearby teammate (only one teammate can be healed at a time). Different colored icons are displayed to Conduit informing the player of teammates' shield health:White: Allies have full shieldGreen: Allies have full temporary shieldYellow: Allies have more than 50% shield, but not full.Red: Allies have less than 50% shield.
