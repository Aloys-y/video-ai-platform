# Stock

Stocksare a group ofattachmentsthat improve the reload speed, handling, and aim drift of most largerweapons. There are two kinds of stock; theSniper Stockfor sniper rifles and marksman weapons, and theStandard Stockfor all else.
## Standard Stock
The Standard Stock can be equipped on allassault rifles,submachine guns,light machine guns, and allshotgunsminus theMozambique Shotgun.
