# Extended Mag

Extended Magsare a group ofattachmentsthat increase the magazine capacity of mostweapons. Eachammo typehas its own Extended Mag, exceptShotgun Shells, which instead has the Shotgun Bolt to bolster fire rate.
## Tiers
- Level 1: small capacity increase.
- Level 2: medium capacity increase.
- Level 3: highest capacity increase.
- Level 4: same asLevel 3, but automatically reloads stowed weapons after 5 seconds.

## Shotgun Bolt
Unlike all other magazines, the Shotgun Bolt does not affect magazine size. Instead, it boosts the fire rate of shotguns.
Weapon | Default |  |  | 
EVA-8 Auto | 2.30 | 2.53 | 2.76 | 2.99
Mastiff Shotgun | 1.10 | 1.21 | 1.27 | 1.32
Mozambique | 2.20 | 2.53 | 2.75 | 2.97
For thePeacekeeper, it instead shortens the time to rechamber.
Weapon | Default |  |  | 
Peacekeeper | 1.10 | 1.02 | 0.96 | 0.94
Rather than passively reloading the whole magazine, theLevel 4 Shotgun Boltautomatically reloads one shot at a time while sliding.
