# Loot

This article is based on data from theNovember 01, 2022 Patch.
Loot refers to the wide array of items available to find in match. Loot can be found scattered around themap, usually placed on the ground or found inSupply Bins.
Item rarities are denoted by their color (Common,Rare,Epic,Legendary,Mythic) and their respective levels (1,2,3,4,5). Most zones on the maps are designated asBasic Tier,Mid TierorHigh Tier, indicating the probability of finding higher-tier items.
A random named zone is also marked as a Hot Zone every match, where the overall loot quality is increased and oneFully Kitted Weaponcan spawn.
Loot currently located inReplicators, exceptShield Batteries,Med Kits, andAmmo, cannot be found on the ground or in Supply Bins.
The following sections list the chance of the various lootable items depending on their group or loot source.

### Center Slot
The center slot of a Care Package contains either aSupply Drop Weaponor a "Jackpot Item". The chances for either to appear depend on which round the Care Package appeared:
- For Round 1 Packages, aSupply Drop Weaponand aJackpot Itemhave the same chance to spawn;
- For the Round 2 Package, aSupply Drop Weaponhas a 75% chance to spawn, while aJackpot Itemhas a 25% chance to spawn;
- For the Round 3 Package, aSupply Drop Weaponis guaranteed to spawn.

## Notes
- Level 3Gearhas a 0.75% chance to spawn asLevel 4Gear instead, regardless of the zone's loot tier.
- OneFully Kitted Weaponhas a 80% chance to spawn in a Hot Zone.
- Some specific, small lootable areas have different loot tables from the rest of the areas: for example, the ring of fire in the middle of Octane'sGauntletonKings Canyonhas a high chance to spawn aLevel 3 Evo Shieldor aFully Kitted Weapon. These special lootable areas ignore the zone's loot tier. Other examples of such guaranteed Ultra Loot spawns are the centre low ground ofHarvesteronWorld's Edgeand the centre of the circular tree cluster east of Containment onKings Canyon.
- TheC.A.R. SMGnever spawns withLight Rounds.
