# Optic

Opticsare a category ofattachmentswhich alter the sights of aweapon. Different optics offer different levels of magnification; some provide a static change, while others are variable and can toggle between different levels with the sprint button (⇧ Left Shift///). Sniper scopes, usable only onsniper rifles, include distance meters and dynamic marks on the reticle to help compensate for bullet drop.Legendary-tier scopes are Digital Threat scopes, which highlight enemies with a red silhouette even when obscured bysmokeorgas.
InBattle Royale, all optics are available as groundloot, with the exception of the4x-10x Digital Sniper Threat, which can only be found inCare Packages. InArenas,Control, and othergame modeswhich grant free selection of weapons, optics can be freely swapped by holding///, provided the weapon's level is high enough to match the optic.
