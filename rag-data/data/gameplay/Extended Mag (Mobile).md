# Extended Mag (Mobile)

Extended Magsare a group ofattachmentsthat increase the magazine capacity of mostweapons, except forshotgunsand theCharge Rifle. Eachammo typehas its own Extended Mag, exceptShotgun Shells.
Numbers highlighted inredare due to the weapon currently being aSupply Drop Weaponwith a temporary mag size increase.
## Tiers
- Level 1: small capacity increase.
- Level 2: medium capacity increase, 5% faster reload.
- Level 3: highest capacity increase, 10% faster reload.
- Level 4: same asLevel 3, but automatically reloads stowed weapons after 5 seconds.
