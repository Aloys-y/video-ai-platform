# Legend Upgrades

Legend Upgradesare special perks introduced inSeason 20to allBattle Royalemodes. These add upgrades to aLegend's abilities as you level up your Evo Armor. Only one option may be chosen per Level-Up for a maximum of two perks total. This feature restarts with each match.
