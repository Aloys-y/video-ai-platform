# Shield

| For other uses, seeShield (disambiguation).
Shieldsare a buffer that absorb damage before alegendstarts losinghealth. Shields are gained by equipping aBody Shield, which grants the player anywhere from 2-4 bars (depending on rarity) or anEvo Shield, which grants 2-5 bars (depending on the Evo stage); each bar absorbs 25 points of damage. They do not automatically regenerate (with the exception ofWattson'sSpark of GeniusandInterception Pylon), but can be replenished by using shield-restoring items or by equipping an undamaged body shield or Evo shield. Furthermore,Finisherssuccessfully executed completely refills your shield health.
Shields do not absorb damage fromthe RingorCaustic's Nox Gas and will damage your health.
## Shield-restoring items
Shield-restoring items can be found across the map. They can be used, when shields are damaged, via the inventory or health kits menu. Using a restoring item takes several seconds and slows down your movement and disables other actions while being used but can be canceled if needed.
The following items restore shields:
- Shield Cell[25 shields (50 withGold Armor), 3 seconds]
- Shield Battery(100% shields, 5 seconds)
- Phoenix Kit(100% shields andhealth, 10 seconds)
