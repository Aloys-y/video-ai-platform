# Crafting

A Replicator.
Craftingis a mechanic introduced inSeason 6. It allows players to craftloot.
## Materials
Players can gainMaterialsby openingSupply Binsor by interacting with Materials Harvesters that randomly generate near aReplicator. Supply Bins grant5to the player that opened them, whereas Harvesters grant25to everyone in the squad. They do not occupy any inventory space and are kept after death (in case the player is respawned). They cannot be looted off death boxes.
Players can also earn materials by clearing Wildlife Nests onStorm Point:90per spider nest,150per prowler nest, and250for the large prowler nest in the Forbidden Zone. These materials are split among the squadmates that are nearby at the time of clearing. However, for the large prowler nest, all players in the area (regardless of squad) gets a share.
## Replicator
Replicator menu.
Replicators are stations that can turn collected materials into loot. Their locations on themapare randomized but players can reveal their locations during the first 10 seconds of the game. Additional Replicators get dropped as the game progresses.
Crafting in a replicator takes 10 seconds, and all players in the nearby area are notified if a Replicator is being used. Replicators have ten slots: two change daily, two change weekly, two change every season and four are permanent.

## Arenas
Main article:Arenas
InArenas, materials are instead used to purchase items and abilities from the shop. Players are given materials for free at the start of each round. Players can also earn materials through combat (75per kill), or by collecting them from the Materials Harvesters spread throughout themap. The Harvesters can each only be used once per round and they give200to an entire team. Even though there areSupply Binsin Arenas, players do not get any materials by opening them.
