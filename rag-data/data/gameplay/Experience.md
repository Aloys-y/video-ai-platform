# Experience

Experience(orXP, for short) is a player levelling mechanic in Apex Legends that allows players to unlockCrafting Metals,Legend TokensandApex Packsby playing the game.
## Gaining Experience
Obtaining experience is based on several different factors. The list below shows how the total experience gained for each match is calculated:
Factor | XP Earned
Won Match | 900 XP
Top 5 Finish | 300 XP
Time Survived | 3 XP per second survived
Kills | 50 XP per kill
Damage Done | 0.25 XP per 1 damage point
Revive Ally | 25 XP per revive
Respawn Ally | 200 XP per respawn
First Kill of the Day | 500 XP
Killed Champion | 500 XP per kill of the champion squad
Kill Leader | 50 XP if the player had been the kill leader in a match at any point
Champion Squad | 500 XP if the player was the champion squad going into a match
Playing with Friends | +5% XP bonus to Time Survived
Playing with Friends (x2) | +10% XP bonus to Time Survived
### XP Boosts
Battle PassXP Boostscan be obtained as a Premium Battle Pass reward or as anEventreward. These add a 10% bonus to Top 5 and Win XP. They stack and apply to everyone in the player's party (up to 300%), and reset everyseason.[1]
## Levels
The player will gain a level every set number of experience. The required experience scales up depending on the current level. Below is a table showing how much XP is required to reach the next level and how much more XP is required for the next level, compared to the previous level.
There are a total of 2000 levels, split into four tiers. After reaching level 500 in one tier, levelling up will reset the level back to level 1 of the next tier, up until level 500 tier 4.

## Rewards
Every time the player levels up a reward is given. This reward will always be600, some levels will also give either anApex PackorCrafting Metalswith the600.
In total, 199Apex Packsare available for free through level 1 - 500 Tier 1. Every subsequent tier will award a total of 115Apex Packs.
After reaching level 500 Tier 4 (the current level cap) the player will still be able to earn600 for every 18,000 experience.
## Trivia
- The Level Cap has been increased twice:BeforeSeason 3the level cap was Level 100, which also had a different badge design that can now only be seen for accounts exactly at Tier 1 Level 100.BeforeSeason 14the level cap was Level 500.
