# Drop-Off

Drop-Off

Gamemode | Arenas
Location | Malta,Psamathe
Drop-Offis a customArenasmapintroduced inSeason 12during theWarriorsCollection Event. The map is located on the planetPsamathe.
## Lore
Sitting high above the skyline of Malta is the fully automated Hammond Manufacturing Tower that was constructed for a single purpose: the early production of critical systems forOlympus.[1]Codenamed Drop-Off, its rooftop is left exposed due to its height and lack of human presence. Brought together by a mutual hatred for big Outland corporations, Arenas fighters covertly commandeer the place at night for fierce Arena matches under the smoggy skies. It’s a win-win for all groups involved, with an added element of danger for the participants and profits made on Hammond’s property right under their nose.[2]
In 2735,Revenant's source code was relocated to Drop-Off. A strike team consisting of Revenant,Lifeline,Mad Maggie,Crypto,Loba, andValkyriebroke into the facility in an attempt to recover or destroy the source code, fighting through a horde ofSpectres,Reapers, andTicks. The source code was then recovered byTorres Silva.[3]
