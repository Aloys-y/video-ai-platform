# ZEUS Station

Z.E.U.S Stationis a location found onStorm Point. It is also used as a standalone map inGun Run,Lockdown, andTeam Deathmatch.
## Gameplay
ZEUS Station is amid tierlootzone found on the north end ofStorm Point. It is a large electrical facility composed of a central cylindrical building and several smaller buildings surrounding it. The area is bordered on the south end by large overhead tubes, on the west and east by mountains, and on the north by a cliff. To the east is a tunnel passageway connecting toLightning Rod, the closest POI. There is a singleRespawn Beaconon the northeast corner, and the map's onlyJump Towerat the southwest corner.
