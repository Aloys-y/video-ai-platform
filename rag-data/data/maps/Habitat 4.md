# Habitat 4

Habitat 4

Habitat 4is a customArenasmapintroduced inSeason 11during theDark Depthsevent.
The map is located on the planetGaea.
## Lore
Storm Pointis just one of many islands in theNew Antilliaarchipelago. Habitat 4 is one of the smallest islands in the chain, and is best known as aLeviathanbreeding ground. EarlyIMCresearch groups were surprised to find that a large population of local Leviathans gravitated toward the island, and designated Habitat 4 as an ethology base to study the creatures' behavior. It's been over a century since then, and while the IMC is gone, the gentle giants they studied here remain.[1]
