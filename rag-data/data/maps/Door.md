# Door

Doorsare found on everymapinApex Legends. They are barriers that block movement and gunfire, and can be opened or closed by interacting with them (E///).
## Standard doors
Standard doors onStorm Point.
Most doors are 2 meters tall and open with a hinge attached to the wall. These doors can be obstructed by players,Death Boxes, or objects placed byabilities, including theNox Gas Trap,Amped Cover,Interception Pylon,Black Market Boutique, orCastle Wall.
Doors are invulnerable to most sources of damage, but the following can be used to damage them:
- Destroys a door in three hits:Bullets from aRevved UpRampage LMG
- Destroys a door in two hits:Melee attacks
- Destroys a door in one hit:Explosives (Frag Grenade,Arc Star,Knuckle Cluster,Missile Swarm,Defensive Bombardment,Rolling Thunder)Bullets fromMobile Minigun "Sheila"APerimeter Securityfence that intersects with a doorAWrecking BallACastle Wall
- Thermite Grenades or aRiot Drilldestroy doors in 2 seconds. Note that the Riot Drill cannot be placed on the door to break it, rather the flame needs to hit the door.
- Any door in the vicinity of aBlack Holewill be destroyed.
- A door struck by aSmoke Launcherwill not appear to take damage, but will be able to be destroyed by a single melee attack afterwards.
UsingCatalyst'sBarricadeon a door will reinforce it, preventing enemies from interacting with it and doubling the amount of damage it can withstand. It can also be used on an empty doorway to create a ferrofluid door.
Hitting the handle of the door with a melee attack,30-30 Repeater,Wingman,sniper rifle, or theL-STAR EMGwill knock it open.
## Large doors
Large doors onBroken MoonandKings Canyon.
There are two variants of larger door; one is a sliding double door, and one is a vertical sliding door which raises when opened. These doors cannot be obstructed or destroyed in any way.
