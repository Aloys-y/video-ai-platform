Repulsor, orRepulsor Station, is a location on the Southeast ofKings Canyon.
## Gameplay
The Repulsor Tower contains both a Survey Beacon and a Ring Console. It should be noted that only the buildings on the east side of the location have High Tier loot, and those on the western half have Mid Tier loot.
## Lore
The Repulsor Tower on Kings Canyon was installed to make the island suitable for use as an arena for theApex Games. Based on the tower used on the planetLeviathan, it emitted a high-pitched frequency to repel the hostile local wildlife, such asFlyersandLeviathans.
In 2733, as part of a revenge plot against theMercenary Syndicate, the hackerTae Joon Park, who had been framed for the murder ofhis foster sister, destroyed the Repulsor Tower by detonating an EMP. Without the tower to protect the island, Flyers and Leviathans swarmed Kings Canyon, making the island temporarily unsuitable for use as an arena. As a result, Apex Games matches on Kings Canyon were suspended and the Syndicate was forced to temporarily relocate to a new site onTalos,World's Edge.
