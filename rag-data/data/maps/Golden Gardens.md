# Golden Gardens

Golden Gardens

Golden Gardens inSeason 7.
Gamemode | Battle Royale,Arenas
Location | Olympus
Golden Gardensis a location found onOlympus. It was also used as a standalone map inArenasinSeason 9.
## Gameplay
Golden Gardens is amid tierlootzone composed of four clusters of small buildings with a small yard in the middle. The eastern cluster sits atop a hill, which can be scaled via sloping bridges over the central yard or via ziplines in the room underneath the hill. The area is enclosed in an oval shape; the western half of the border is made of a long pond, while the eastern half is made of a hedge wall. There is aRespawn Beaconbehind the southern cluster of buildings.
