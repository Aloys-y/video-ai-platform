Rift Aftermathis a location inOlympus.
A circular basin with numerous destroyed structures around it, this point of interest favors long-range fights due to the long sightlines.
## Lore
Main article:Phase Runner accident
The Rift Aftermath is the result of an explosion of the Phase energy contained within aPhase Runner. The specific Phase Runner in this case being the one inside the Peck Laboratories used byProject IrisonOlympus.
The Rift has became a tourist attraction since the "accident" and has stayed as such for the next 40 years. The Rift has become unstable overtime and due to this, Olympus was fully evacuated sometime in 2733.
