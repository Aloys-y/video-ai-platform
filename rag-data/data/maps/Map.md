# Map

Mapsare the locations and arenas in which matches take place. Maps vary in size and shape depending on thegame modethey are meant for;Battle Royalemaps are the largest, contain the most uniquefeatures, and often get substantial updates at the beginning of aseason. Game modes likeControl,Gun Run, orTeam Deathmatchuse smaller maps, many of which are points of interest (POIs) repurposed from Battle Royale maps.
