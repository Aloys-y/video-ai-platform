Harvesteris a location inWorld's Edge. It was added inSeason 4, replacing Fuel Depot and The High Ground.
## Lore
In 2733, Hammond Robotics installed a large Planet Harvester within the arena of World's Edge to extract valuable materials such as branthium from the crust of Talos. As time went on, the Harvester's work strengthened, increasing the strain on the planet and causing a sharp, sudden increase in seismic activity. Eventually, in 2734, the Harvester overloaded, self-destructing and causing catastrophic damage to the landscape of World's Edge. Many fissures opened up across the landscape, leading Hammond Robotics to performatively install theLava SiphonandClimatizerto make it seem like they were fixing their error.
