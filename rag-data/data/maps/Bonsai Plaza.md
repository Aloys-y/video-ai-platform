## Lore
Bonsai Plaza is a metropolitan area in the floating city of Olympus, containing many prestigious restaurants.
In 2708, the simulacrum hitmanRevenantattacked one such restaurant, Tenmei, on a contract to eliminate a notorious thief namedMarcos Andrade. Marcos, his wifeAlanza, and several security guards were killed during the attack, and the couple's young daughter,Loba, was orphaned.[1]This event was witnessed byPathfinder, who was working as a chef at Tenmei at the time.[2]
