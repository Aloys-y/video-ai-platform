Downed Beastis a location inStorm Point.
## Lore
Officially designated (probably byECHO) asUnknown 045, this large crustacean attackedStorm PointduringBangalore'slastApex Gamesmatch. After it was killed by theLegends, a small ECHO camp was established around it for the cleanup of its remains. This small camp and the cleanup effort was visited by theSyndicate'sleaderEduardo Silva.
Meat from its remains are being sold in the black market.[1]
