# Party Crasher

Party Crasher

Gamemode | Arenas,Team Deathmatch
Location | Kómma,Solace
Codename | mp_rr_party_crasher
Party Crasheris a customArenasmapintroduced inSeason 9located on the planetSolace.
Its main feature is the crashedMirage Voyage, which makes up half of the map. The opposite half contains a two-story building with a central staircase. These two sides are connected by a low bridge.
## Lore
While taking theMirage Voyageon a joyride through the former IMC controlled hotspot Kómma on planet Solace,Miragelost control of the ship and crashed it into a plaza. Any other person would have spent the rest of their life paying the bill for such a blunder, but since this once thriving city full of gambling, clubs, and home to various questionable activities has become a ghost town, Mirage, the Apex Legend he is, struck a deal with the current owners. With support of the upcoming Arenas matches, Mirage cleared his debt by assisting to reignite the city and put it back on the map.[1]
