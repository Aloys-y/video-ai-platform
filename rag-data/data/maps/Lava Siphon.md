# Lava Siphon

Lava Siphon

Gamemode | Battle Royale,Control
Location | World's Edge
Lava Siphonis a location found onWorld's Edge. It is also used as a standalone map inControl. It was added inSeason 10, replacingSorting Factory.
## Gameplay
Lava Siphon is ahigh tierlootzone composed of three large multi-story buildings linked to each other with gondolas, and one large building built into a nearby mountain. Each building has a field of lava between them and verticalziplineson the exteriors so players can return to the interior if they fall. The area has aRespawn Beaconon its southwest corner, aJump Toweron its northeast, and manySupply Bins.
## Lore
The overloading of the Harvester at World's Edge wreaked havoc on the ecosystem of Talos. A major symptom of this was the opening of massive lava fissures across the surface of the planet. In order to mitigate this damage, Hammond Robotics constructed the Lava Siphon.[1]This construct was publicly intended to extract excess lava and harmlessly release it into the atmosphere.[2]However, this proved to simply be a performative measure, as the Lava Siphon truly only heated the air.[3]
