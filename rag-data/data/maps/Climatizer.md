Climatizeris a location inWorld's Edge. It was added inSeason 10, replacing Refinery.
## Lore
The overloading of theHarvesterat World's Edge wreaked havoc on the ecosystem of Talos. A major symptom of this was the opening of massive lava fissures across the surface of the planet. In order to mitigate this damage,Hammond Roboticsconstructed the Climatizer.[1]This construct was publicly intended to release heat-absorbing aerosols to help with local climate control.[2]However, this proved to simply be a performative measure, as the Climatizer truly only cooled the air to offset the excess heat produced by theLava Siphon.[3]
