# Loot Bunker

“ | Defunct IMC bunker. Excavated by Syndicate. Gear cache for game-makers.
A closed loot bunker.
Loot Bunkerswere aMap Featureadded toKings CanyoninSeason 5. There were five scattered across themap, but only four of them could be opened. They were removed inSeason 8.
Each of the four openable loot bunkers had two hatches: one large hatch that could only be opened from the outside and one small hatch that could only be opened from the inside. Within the loot bunkers were Terminal Stations with a charge tower and a fixedLegendaryitem (either a Level 4gearor afully kitted weapon) that rotated eachseason.
Cryptohad a special interaction with three of the four loot bunkers. At the end of the hallway, on the opposite wall of the panel used to open the small hatch, there is a vent. If Crypto uses his drone to go into the vent, the drone will reach a room with several monitors; the drone can interact with the monitors to play a message fromMila Alexander, explaining what happened to her after "Forever Family".

## The Lost Tomb
“ |
— Crypto on the bunker he marked as "???".
This bunker is located by Slum Lakes. It cannot be opened, except at the end of theSeason 5quest, "The Broken Ghost" (which is no longer available).
After theLegendshave assembled the artifact for the quest,Lobaleads them to the bunker and it opens for them. The location is revealed to beThe Lost Tombwith the subtitleA Secret Place. The bunker is unlike any of the others: it appears to still be in use and there is vegetation growing inside.
In the end, there is an active charge tower with a headless simulacrum body attached to it. The player completes the quest by attaching the artifact,Ash's head, onto the body, and Ash is revived.
Gameplay footage of the finale of "The Broken Ghost" quest. The mission "Ashes to Ashes" begins at 13:30.
## Trivia
The bunkers are marked in Crypto's Map Room.
- Although the bunkers were added to Kings Canyon at the start ofSeason 5, they could not be opened until theLost Treasuresevent. Despite this, Terminal Station F-85 was still accessible because it does not need a hatch to enter.
- InCrypto's Map Room, the bunkers are given nicknames in Korean. They can be combined to form the Korean saying "Go-saeng kkeut-e nag-i on-da" (고생 끝에 낙이 온다), which translates to "At the end of hardship comes happiness."
