# Skull Town

Skull Town

Gamemode | Team Deathmatch,Gun Run
Location | Kings Canyon
Codename | mp_rr_freedm_skulltown
Skull Townis amaplocated withinKings Canyon. It was part of theBattle Royalemap until its destruction inSeason 5, but has since returned as a standalone map inArenas,Gun Run, andTeam Deathmatch.
## Lore
Skull Town was a crude settlement established on Kings Canyon near theThunderdomearena, helping to house the IMC's workers and those who came to fight and spectate in the Thunderdome.
AfterLoba Andradedetonated a facility beneath Kings Canyon holding duplicates of the simulacrum hitmanRevenant, Skull Town was destroyed, crashing into the Duchess River.
The area where it used to stand now exists asRelic.
## Trivia
- During theGenesisCollection Event, Skull Town temporarily returned as anArenasmap. This was only temporary, however, as running the map required rendering the entirety of the original version of Kings Canyon.
