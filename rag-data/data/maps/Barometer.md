# Barometer

Barometer

Gamemode | Battle Royale,Control
Location | Storm Point
Barometeris a location found onStorm Point. It is also used as a standalone map inControl.
## Gameplay
Barometer is amid tierlootzone composed of a large island in a lagoon, with several buildings scattered across it. The main observation tower is surrounded by a rotating platform. To its southeast is a research tower with a high upper level accessible byzipline, which also contains aRespawn Beacon. The island is connected to the surrounding land by four bridges and threeGravity Cannons.
