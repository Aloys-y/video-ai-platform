# Pathfinder's Fight Night

Pathfinder's Fight Night

Pathfinder's Fight Nightwas added in the7.2 Updateas part of theFight Nightevent.
It is based on the "Fight Night" short.
## Layout
Located between Docks and Turbine, Pathfinder's Fight Night is a boxing arena with loot rollers inside, filled with high-tier loot. Players who enter the ring cannot use theirweaponsorabilities; in other words, they can only melee. Special animations are played while inside the ring. Any outside projectiles, abilities, orHolo-Sprays will not enter the ring.
