# Overflow

Overflow

Gamemode | Arenas,Team Deathmatch
Location | Hephaestus Station,Talos
Codename | mp_rr_party_crasher
Overflowis a customArenasmapintroduced in theThrillseekersevent located on the planetTalos. Some time after Arenas' removal, it became playable inTeam Deathmatch.
## Lore
After trace amounts of Branthium were discovered in magma flows onTalos, Hammond Robotics began aggressively pursuing various means of mining it. One was an automated processing rig--officially referred to as Hephaestus Station, it’s affectionately referred to as Overflow. It hasn't seen any human activity for the majority of its operation, but after its spacious platforms caught the eyes of theArenasorganizers, it began doing double-duty as an industrial mining operation and a site for chaotic combat.[1]
