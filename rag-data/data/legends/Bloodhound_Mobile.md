Bloodhoundis aReconLegend. They[2][3][4]are unlocked by default.
Bloodhound is a fast-paced Legend great at pushing the enemy in their base. TheirTrackercan be used to find where enemies have gone, andEye of the Allfatherwill reveal hidden enemies and traps. Lastly,Beast of the Huntcauses Bloodhound to gain speed and highlights their enemies.

## Perks
Bloodhound has 3 Perks, 3 Finisher Perks, and 3 Ability Perks
### Perks
- Hunger- Knocking down an enemy resets the cooldown forEye of the Allfather.
- Hunting Instinct- When you ping tracks, your raven will fly towards the enemy squad that left them.
- Memories of the Land- Investigating tracks will show you more information from the enemy.
### Finisher Perks
- Battle Adaptation- Using your Finisher adds 100 damage to yourEvo Shield's level.
- Deadly Momentum- Using your Finisher reduces your Ultimate's cooldown by 30%.
- Interrogator- Using your Finisher reveals the location of the enemy's Squad on the minimap.
### Ability Perks
- Unending Bloodlust- Killing enemies will also increase the duration of your Ultimate.
- Heightened Awareness-Eye of the Allfatherbecomes a spherical scan, but the range is reduced.
- Mortal Wisdom- Your Ultimate reveals the Armor and Health of your enemy but no longer increases your speed.
## Lore
Main article:Bloodhound (character)
“ | Bloodhound is known across the Outlands as one of the greatest game hunters the Frontier has ever seen. The child of two engineers stationed at the New Dawn industrial plant on Talos, Bloodhound was taken in by their uncle Artur after a meltdown destroyed the facility and killed both their parents. Artur taught them the Old Ways, a belief system that focuses on the glory of nature and rejects modern technology. Yet Bloodhound was constantly drawn to technological marvels, and ultimately used both new and old methods to take down a Goliath that preyed on the people of their village, forever changing their life’s path.After Bloodhound’s lover was unceremoniously killed in the Thunderdome, they vowed to win enough battles that they could both enter Valhalla… and the Apex Games were the perfect place for Bloodhound to accumulate victories. Their unparalleled tracking skills are a boon to any team they join, helping them root out hidden opponents and track enemy movements. Calling on Earth’s Old Norse Gods to guide them, Bloodhound believes that destiny is a path that has already been laid out, eventually carrying all to their death. But with that knowledge comes strength, because until that day comes, Bloodhound knows they can’t be stopped.
