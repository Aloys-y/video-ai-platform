Forgewas aminor characteroriginally announced as aLegend[3]forSeason 4. In "Up Close and Personal", he was assassinated byRevenant, who was then announced as theSeason 4Legend.
## Lore
Standing 6'5.5" tall and weighing 269lbs, James McCormick, nicknamed "The Forge", was a 5-time Hyperfighting Federation champion[4]before he was set to join theApex Games. He has already achieved widespread popularity as the "People's Champ", and was notably already popular three years ago when he met, and sexually harassed,Anita Williams[5]. His entrance to the games was highly anticipated because of his popularity, alongside his sponsorship byHammond Robotics.
Before his official entrance to the games, he was being interviewed live by Outland Television'sLisa StonewhenRevenantsnuck behind him and impaled him, immediately causing his death at the age of 28.
After his death, the Forge Museum was opened on Gaea. However, it quickly filed for bankruptcy.[6]
## Datamining
Forge was never intended to be a realLegendand was created solely to deceive data miners, who had already figured out thatRevenantwas going to be theSeason 4Legend. No model or kit was ever developed for Forge. Fake concept art and abilities were purposely leaked into the game's files to give the misdirection more credibility.[3]
- Unlike many of the other leaked Legends who have been discovered for some time, Forge seemingly came out of nowhere with theNovember 05, 2019 Patch.

## Trivia
- Forge's fake abilities bear a striking resemblance to those ofDoomfistfromOverwatch.Many of Forge's fake abilities also have corresponding functions with theBuster Sword R2R5.
- When Forge was announced, his description on theofficial websitewas "Confident yet humble, Forge used his size, strength, and heavy-duty Shatter Gauntlets to batter his opponents into submission."
- In the Sorting Factory ofWorld's Edge, the set-up for Forge's interview could be seen after theSeason 4announcement. Areddeath box later appeared after Forge got assassinated, which only contained a special commemorative charm called "Steel Cut", which after you picked it up, you would keep it forever. As of now, the death box has disappeared. The charm itself is his medallion and is the only in-game item that is related to him and can be obtained.
- The Forge from the "Up Close and Personal" animated short has many physical differences from the Legend artwork: he has a scar above his left eye, he is missing scars on his right arm, and his eyes are brown instead of green. Combined with the in-game description of Forge's charm that says that he is often imitated, this led to speculation that the "real" Forge may still be alive. Since the trailer, there have been multiple conflicting tweets from Respawn employees, some stated that he is in fact dead[7], while some others tease that this may not be the case[8].
