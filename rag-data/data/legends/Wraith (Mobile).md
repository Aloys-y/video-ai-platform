Wraithis anOffensiveLegend. She is unlocked by leveling up to level 15.
A versatile Legend, Wraith can reposition and flank enemies with her abilities. Wraith's tactical abilityInto the Voidallows her to reposition or take cover while being invincible for 4 seconds. Wraith's ultimate abilityDimensional Riftplaces an entrance and exit portal that allows her squad to be safely transported over long distances. Her passive abilityVoices from the Voidgives her and her teammates audible warnings about enemies aiming at them.

## Perks
Wraith has 3 Perks, 3 Finisher Perks, and 3 Ability Perks.
### Perks
- Threat Intel- Voices of the Void displays the distance between you and enemies.
- Escape Void- While knocked down, you can still use Into the Void.
- Phantom Footfall- Slightly increases movement speed when not holding a weapon.
### Finisher Perks
- Battle Adaptation- Using your Finisher adds 100 damage to yourEvo Shield's level.
- Mag Subscription- Using your Finisher refills the ammo of your active weapon and increases reloading speed for 8s.
- Tactical Advantage- Using your Finisher charges your Tactical.
### Ability Perks
- Dissipate- Your Ultimate is available at 50%, but activating it at this point will result in a lesser portal.
- Long Road Ahead- Into the Void's duration increases by 1s, but the cooldown increases by 10s.
- Phase Haste- Movement speed within the Void increases, but the duration decreases.
