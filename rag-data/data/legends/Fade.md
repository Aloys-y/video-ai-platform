## Perks
Fade has 3 Perks, 3 Finisher Perks, and 3 Ability Perks.
### Perks
- Renewed Purpose- Exiting Phase Space triggers a Surge.
- Lingering Shadow- Surge leaves behind after-images that Squadmates can touch for a speed boost.
- Punisher's Remorse- You can Flash Back when knocked down.
### Finisher Perks
- Battle Adaptation- Using your Finisher adds 100 points to yourEvo Shield's total.
- Tactical Advantage- Using your Finisher recharges your Tactical.
- Mag Subscription- Using your Finisher refills the ammo of your active weapon and increases reloading speed for 8s.
### Ability Perks
- Pact of the Suit- When Tactical Ability Flash Back is active and the shield is empty, the shield will be slightly restored.
- Phase Echo- Flash Back leaves behind after-images that Squadmates can touch for a speed boost.
- Lost in the Void- Your squad can stay in a Phase Chamber longer, but enemies in the Phase Chamber are no longer scanned.
## Lore
Main article:Fade (character)
“ | Born into a family of military-tech hunters, Ignacio Huamaní learned early to never let loot go to your head. He worked beside his ex-mercenary father and brothers, recovering advanced weaponry to sell to the highest bidder. But as the youngest, he was always stuck with the role of lookout. Ignacio was eager to prove his worth, so he secretly connected with a client offering an opportunity too good to turn down: a hefty reward for finding a lost prototype tech suit.It wasn't easy locating the crashed IMC cargo ship containing the suit, but when Ignacio finally did, he ended up walking his family into an ambush. The contract was a set-up, and his family paid the price. But before the client's hitmen could kill Ignacio, he activated the suit and phased to another dimension. It took him a long time to master enough of the suit's phasing power to return to his home dimension, but when he did, all that remained of his family was dried blood.Ignacio joins the Apex Games as Fade to enact his revenge. He'll climb the ranks, drawing as much attention to himself and his suit as possible to lure the client out of hiding. The suit will be the bait -- Fade will be the punishment.
