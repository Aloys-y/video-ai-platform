# Teasers

Teaseris a general term for sniplets of images, audio recordings or videos that were released on the game's official social media channels, where the main purpose is to tease events or next seasons, with some lore contents. They are either made in-house byRespawn Entertainmentor commissioned from members of theApex Legendscommunity. This content is considered canon to theloreof Apex Legends.
This page lists them by order of release and does not includeStories from the Outlands,comics,motion comics, paid content likePathfinder's Quest, or any of the trailers.

## Season 15 Teasers
The following images serve as a lead up to Season 15.
- Seer and Octane stand at a cluttered round table containing a map of Boreas, concepting what the Apex Games would look like. “Octane’s Town Takeover Parte Dos” has been stuck on the map. A draft of Seer’s proposal speech to Duardo Silva to bring the Apex Games to Boreas can be seen beneath the map. There are also some of Octane’s belongings scattered on the table, as well as writing utensils and art supplies. Various takeout food items and beverages can be seen, including energy drinks, coffee, and juice. Seer’s phone on the table shows an upcoming appointment with Duardo Silva’s office and missed calls from Rampart and his mother (labeled "nne").Released on October 7, 2022.[49]
- Duardo works on his computer at his meticulously organized desk. His black gloves and green glasses are placed to the side. On the monitor, there is a contract between entities including the Syndicate, the CRC (Cleo Recovery Council), and the NAB (National Alliance of Boreas). Behind the monitor, Seer and Octane’s map proposal for the Apex Games can be seen. Some landscape photos from an unknown location are placed upon the desk, as well as Duardo’s phone which shows an email from Octane and recent calls to Seer, Jacob Young, and Lisa Stone. Duardo’s cane is also on the desk with its handle removed, revealing that it holds an injector with a green liquid inside.Released on October 9, 2022.[50]
- An image of a desk littered with crystals, tarot cards, and a tablet in dim lighting. On the tablet, three incoming messages are visible. The first one is an expanded email from Duardo Silva inviting Legends to the new home of the Apex Games. The second email is an unopened message from Rose asking forCatalystto call her back. The third is an email CRC's Human resource expressing thanks for Catalyst's interest in working for CRC, and requested reference from her..Released on October 13, 2022.[51]
- A poster of a green and blue planet is shown on a dark background with a watermark of National Assembly of Boreas. The planet (implies to beBoreas) is breaking off into pieces shaped like butterflies and has a caption underneath that reads: "Doing your part creates a butterfly effect. We can save this planet together."Released on October 14, 2022.[52]
- A poster featuring a starry background and bright red stripes. A group of people can be seen holding up a destroyed moon, but the moon has an iris and pupil making it to look like an eyeball. At the bottom the caption reads "Save our moon and save ourselves! We look to you."Released on October 15, 2022.[53]
- A poster with three hands clasped over a tan background can be seen. The headline reads "your contribution is out of this world!" and "every hand is a helping hand" can be seen at the bottom of the poster in red and blue text.Released on October 17, 2022.[54]
