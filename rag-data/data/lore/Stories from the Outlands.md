# Stories from the Outlands

Stories from the Outlandsare a series of animated featurettes about the lore ofApex Legends.
Starting withSeason 2and with the exception ofSeason 16, one is released before the start of eachseasonthat introduces the nextLegend. Meanwhile, longer Stories from the Outlands are released sparingly; they are usually accompanied byeventsandTown Takeovers.
